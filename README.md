# carbon-cost

What carbon and road charges does a truck trip pay? carbon-cost works it out per country and per charge, and shows the carbon part separately:

- **Km-based road tolls:** Denmark (vejafgift), Germany (Lkw-Maut), Austria (GO-Maut), the Netherlands (vrachtwagenheffing), Belgium (kilometerheffing, by region), Switzerland and Liechtenstein (LSVA), Czechia (mýtné), Poland (e-TOLL), Bulgaria (BG TOLL), Slovenia (DarsGo), Slovakia (mýto), Hungary (HU-GO) and Romania (TollRo), split into their legal parts (infrastructure, air, noise and CO2) where the law gives them.
- **Entry-to-exit motorway tolls:** Croatia (Hrvatske autoceste and Bina-Istra), Serbia (Putevi Srbije), Montenegro (Monteput) and France (APRR, AREA, ADELAC, ALIAE, VINCI Autoroutes' ASF, Escota and Cofiroute, Sanef and SAPN, A'liénor, the Millau viaduct, ATMB's A40, Atlandes, ALBEA, SFTRF's A43, Alicorne, ALiS and the Normandie and Tancarville bridges), priced from the station where the route enters a toll system to the one where it leaves.
- **Time-based road user charges:** Latvia (autoceļu lietošanas nodeva), Lithuania (kelių naudotojo mokestis), Estonia (teekasutustasu), the Eurovignette in Sweden (vägavgift) and Luxembourg (droit d'usage), and the UK HGV levy, priced per day the trip spends in the country by its passage times (a calendar day in Sweden, Luxembourg and the UK, 24 hours in Estonia and Lithuania; Latvia at one day). Lithuania counts the motor vehicle's own axles: give `motor_axles` (e.g. 2 for a tractor).
- **Motorway sections charged per passage:** Portugal's lanços (IMT's table), each in its middle.
- **Toll stations charged per passage:** Norway's AutoPASS stations (bompenger), in the direction each charges and once per hour-rule group; Ireland's toll plazas (M50 and the PPP roads); Sweden's bridge charges (Motala, Sundsvall, Skuru) and the Stockholm and Göteborg congestion taxes by time of day; North Macedonia's toll stations; Montenegro's Sozina tunnel; Greece's motorway toll stations (Olympia Odos, Nea Odos, Kentriki Odos, Moreas, Aegean Motorway) and the Rio–Antirrio bridge; Turkey's KGM bridges (15 Temmuz Şehitler, Fatih Sultan Mehmet, Osmangazi, Yavuz Sultan Selim).
- **Bridge tolls:** Storebælt and Øresund, a fixed price per crossing by the truck's length (`length_m`, default 16.5 m).
- **Area charges per day** (`area_charge`): London's Congestion Charge, when the route is in the zone in its charging hours (with a departure `time`; without one, assumed).
- **Tunnels and bridges charged per passage**, on top of the countries' own tolls: the Herrentunnel (Lübeck), the Warnowtunnel (Rostock), the Westerscheldetunnel, the Liefkenshoektunnel (Antwerp), the Great St Bernard tunnel, the Dartford Crossing, the Tyne Tunnels, the M6 Toll, the Humber, Mersey and Tamar bridges and the Kiltunnel, each in the direction it charges.
- **Electronic toll discounts:** give `etc` when the truck pays by toll box or account agreement: Croatia's ENC (21.74%, plus 3% EURO V/EEV or 7% EURO VI on HAC; 30% on Bina-Istra), Storebælt's Grøn rabat (13%, EURO VI or zero-emission), and ØresundBUSINESS prices.
- **EU ETS on ferry crossings:** the cargo's share of the crossing's emissions × the allowance price.
- **ETS2 on road fuel** from 2028: the trip's fuel emissions × the ETS2 price.
- **National CO2 prices on diesel** (`fuel_co2`): Germany's BEHG (nEHS), Austria's NEHG, Ireland's carbon tax, Portugal's adicionamento sobre as emissões de CO2 and Slovenia's CO2 okoljska dajatev, on the trip's road fuel emissions in the country where the fuel is bought (`fuel_country`, else split by each country's road km).

The route, the km per country and the emissions come from [Sootcase](https://github.com/majidkabir/sootcase) (GLEC Framework and ISO 14083). The rates come from the laws, and each rate file names the law and the page it uses. There are no estimates or fitted factors: every number is a published rate, a price you give, or Sootcase's result.

## Example

```
$ carbon-cost calc -from DKCPH -to DEHAM -cargo-kg 20000 -mass-kg 40000 -axles 5 -euro 6 -co2-class 1 -eua 75
Trip on 2026-11-03: 300.0 km (19.0 km by ferry)
     Charge  Country            Part         Basis           Rate     Amount    EUR
  road_toll       DE  infrastructure      141.0 km   0.155 EUR/km  21.86 EUR  21.86
  road_toll       DE             air      141.0 km   0.023 EUR/km   3.24 EUR   3.24
  road_toll       DE           noise      141.0 km   0.012 EUR/km   1.69 EUR   1.69
  road_toll       DE             co2      141.0 km   0.158 EUR/km  22.28 EUR  22.28
  road_toll       DK  infrastructure      140.0 km    0.44 DKK/km  61.60 DKK   8.26
  road_toll       DK             co2      140.0 km     0.6 DKK/km  84.00 DKK  11.26
  road_toll       DK       air_noise      140.0 km    0.06 DKK/km   8.40 DKK   1.13
  ferry_ets                      co2  0.030 t CO2e  75 EUR/t CO2e   2.25 EUR   2.25
Total: EUR 71.97, of which carbon EUR 35.79
```

The numbers are this repo's worked test case (`cost/cost_test.go`).

### Compare trucks

`-compare 1,4,5` prices the same trip for trucks in other CO2 classes, and `-trips-per-year` turns the savings per trip into savings per year:

```
$ carbon-cost calc -from DKCPH -to DEHAM -cargo-kg 20000 -eua 75 -compare 1,4,5 -trips-per-year 200
...
  CO2 class  Tolls EUR  Total EUR  Carbon EUR  Saving/trip  Saving/year
          1      69.72      71.97       35.79         0.00
          4      48.82      51.07       19.02        20.90      4180.00
          5       2.44       4.69        2.25        67.28     13456.00
```

The route and the cargo's emissions stay the same. The exception is a class 5 (zero-emission) truck, which burns no road fuel and so pays no ETS2. In 2026 the class 5 truck pays only the DK class 5 rate, because Germany exempts it.

## Things to know

- **Tolled roads:** Where the tool has no road classes for a country (see Road types), all its km are counted as tolled by default. In Denmark the 2025–2027 tolled network covers about 100% of foreign trucks' driving and about 70% of Danish trucks' ([Skatteministeriet's answer to Folketinget](https://eu.dk/samling/20222/lovforslag/l74/spm/35/svar/1958301/2708151.pdf)), and from 2028 every public road is tolled. Germany tolls motorways and federal roads. If part of a trip is on untolled roads, give `tolled_share`.
- **Regional tolls:** where a country's toll differs by region (Belgium), the tool prices the km per region from Sootcase's `region_km` (ISO 3166-2, e.g. `BE-VLG`). `country_km` still covers the whole country. `tolled_share` and `env_zone_km` take a region code or a country code. 
- **Environmental zones:** the Danish surcharge applies to the km you give in `env_zone_km`.
- **Ferry ETS:** this is the ETS cost of the cargo's share of the crossing. On a crossing the tool knows (Rødby–Puttgarden, Gedser–Rostock), and when you give the truck's `tare_kg`, the emissions come from that crossing's own ships in the EU MRV report: their freight CO2e per tonne-nautical mile × (truck + cargo) × the crossing's distance. MRV counts the vehicle in the freight mass, which is why the tare is needed. Otherwise the emissions come from Sootcase's ferry part, which uses the GLEC ro-pax default. The share of emissions under the ETS is 40% for 2024, 70% for 2025 and 100% from 2026, all for crossings between EU/EEA ports. A ferry line's own surcharge is based on its ships' real emissions and its own pricing.
- **Times and directions:** with a departure `time` (local time where the trip starts; each station is timed in its own country's zone), each toll station is taken to be reached after its km along the route at `avg_speed_kmh` (default 70, an assumption, not a law). That time prices congestion taxes per passage (with their daily caps and Göteborg's 60-minute rule, which charges the highest amount once) and the hours a toll system or station is free. With `driver_rest`, the times include one driver's 45-minute break after 4.5 hours' driving and 11-hour daily rest after 9 hours ([Regulation (EC) No 561/2006](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/561/oj) Arts. 6–8); the web page turns it on. The times also count the days of time-based charges. Stations with a heading charge only the direction they face; entry–exit tariffs published per direction (Turkey's concessions, Spain's SEITT) are priced entry to exit, never the other way's price.
- **Fuel CO2:** national CO2 prices on diesel are paid where the fuel is bought, which the route doesn't tell. Give `fuel_country`; otherwise the road fuel is split by each country's road km (an assumption). Countries that tax carbon per litre (DK, SE, FI, NO, LT) aren't included, since that needs litres, which Sootcase's tonne-km factors don't give.
- **Prices:** EUA and ETS2 prices are yours to give, since they move every day. DKK converts at the ERM II central rate of 7.46038 unless you give `dkk_per_eur`. CHF, CZK, PLN, HUF, RON, SEK, NOK, GBP and TRY convert at the latest [ECB euro reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html) unless you give `fx` (units per euro, e.g. `{"CHF": 0.93}`).
- **Road types:** Czechia, Poland, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Hungary and Romania charge less off motorways and don't toll local roads. The tool reads the road class of each km of the route from OpenStreetMap (embedded in `roads/data`, built by `tools/roads`): the toll law's motorway class (CZ D, PL A and S, BG A, SK D and R, HU M, RO A and DEx), other national roads (1–2 digit refs; RO DN), and local roads, which aren't charged. Hungary's air and noise charge also takes each km's suburban share from its toll section (OpenStreetMap relations tagged `ref:HU:edid`). `motorway_share` and `tolled_share` override this. A route point counts as on a road within 40 m of it; km counted as local that pass within 150 m of a tolled road are reported in a note, since they may be on it.
- **Past dates:** `date` picks the tables in force that day, and euro rates are the ECB's for that day (embedded since 2024). Rate tables go back to 1 January 2024 for DK, DE, AT, NL, BE (all three regions), IE, PT, LT, HU, MK, CH, LI, CZ, PL, SK, SE, LU, GB, EE, NO (every NVDB version of each station), BG (each version of the Тарифа in Държавен вестник), SI (each DarsGo price list, rail surcharge and Karavanke toll from PISRS), the Mont Blanc and Fréjus tunnels and the Storebælt and Øresund bridges, LV, and RO (the per-day rovinieta of OG 15/2002 until 30 September 2026, with the Danube bridge tolls from 8 September 2025, then TollRo). FR goes back to 2024 for the operators whose tariff documents the Internet Archive kept (APRR, AREA, ASF, Cofiroute, Escota, ALIAE, A'liénor, each table names its own), GR to 2024 except Aegean Motorway, HR to 2024 except Bina-Istra, ES (SEITT) to 2025 except the AP-41, TR to 2024 (the motorways whose 2024 tariffs the Internet Archive kept; the Bosphorus bridges' 2024 tariffs weren't), RS to 10 July 2024 with gaps (see the test); the others start at the date in the table above. `rates/history_test.go` lists the gaps; `tools/history` rebuilds the past tables from the laws, ECB data and archived copies of official pages.
- **Provenance:** each response has a `provenance` block: the release, the Sootcase server and its factor dataset, each rate table used with its file in `rates/data` and the OpenStreetMap extract its stations were placed from, the extract of the road classes, the MRV publication of a ferry crossing, the exchange rates, and the defaults taken (`assumed`). With the release and the request, that repeats the result.

## Use

```
go install github.com/majidkabir/carbon-cost/cmd/carbon-cost@latest
carbon-cost calc -h
carbon-cost serve -addr :8090   # web page at /, POST /v1/carbon-cost, GET /v1/rates, GET /v1/places?q=
```

Or with Docker (images are published for each release tag):

```
docker run -p 8090:8090 -e SOOTCASE_URL=https://sootcase.org -e SOOTCASE_API_KEY=... ghcr.io/majidkabir/carbon-cost
```

Release binaries for Linux, macOS and Windows are on the [releases page](https://github.com/majidkabir/carbon-cost/releases). To release, change the version in `RELEASE`; merging that to main publishes it.

`calc` and `serve` call `https://sootcase.org` by default. Set `SOOTCASE_URL` to use another server, and set `SOOTCASE_API_KEY` to send an API key. The server must run Sootcase v0.9.0 or later, which adds `country_km`.

API request:

```json
{
  "from": {"code": "DKCPH"},
  "to": {"code": "DEHAM"},
  "date": "2026-11-03",
  "cargo_kg": 20000,
  "truck": {"max_mass_kg": 40000, "axles": 5, "euro": 6, "co2_class": 1, "tare_kg": 15000},
  "eua_eur_per_t": 75
}
```

Optional fields: `time` (HH:MM departure, local time where the trip starts; each station's time is taken in its own country's time zone: each toll station is reached that much later at `avg_speed_kmh`, default 70; this prices congestion taxes, the hours a toll is free and the days of time-based charges), `avg_speed_kmh`, `driver_rest`, `vehicle_type` (Sootcase), `tolled_share`, `motorway_share`, `env_zone_km`, `ferry_ets_share`, `ets2_eur_per_t`, `fuel_country`, `dkk_per_eur`, `fx`, `night`, `compare_co2_classes` (e.g. `[1, 4, 5]`, giving `compare[]` in the response) and `trips_per_year`. Each line of the response carries `source` (the law or tariff) and `sources` (links to it).
