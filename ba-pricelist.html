Pricelist and vehicle category

Pricelist and vehicle category

The Government of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, at its 245th session, held on November 26, 2020. The Decree amending the Decree on the collection of the fee for the use of the A1 SN 86/20 motorway was adopted on, pursuant to Articles 1, 2 and 3 of the Regulation on the Charges for the Use of the Motorway A1 (“Official Gazette of FB&H” No. 54/15) ) which entered into force on 16 November 2015 and the Rules amending the Rules on the Toll Collection System Highways and toll-collection facilities in FB&H (“Official Gazette of FB&H” No. 53/15) ), it is determined the charge and the amount of the fee for using the motorway on the constructed sections of the motorway A1:

SECTION SVILAJ – ODZAK

 

SECTION SARAJEVO SJEVER – ZENICA JUG

SECTION SARAJEVO ZAPAD – TARČIN

SECTION MEĐUGORJE – LJUBUŠKI

 

Pursuant to Article 6 of the Rulebook on the Toll Collection System on Highways, Highways and Installed Installations (“Official Gazette FB&H”, No. 53/15), vehicles are classified for the purpose of the collection according to classes:

Motor vehicles with two axles up to the 1.3 m measured at first axle (motorcycles, passenger cars registered up to 5 seats and light delivery vehicles).

Motor vehicles with two or more axles and height measured at the first axis up to the 1.3 m (vans, cars with trailers, caravans with a height of 1.3 m on the front axle and light delivery vehicles with trailer caravan, two-axle vehicles such as the vans without trailers, vehicles with a maximum permissible weight of up to 3,500 kg and vehicles registered for the number of seats from 6 to 9).

Vehicles with two or three axles and height measured at the first axis higher than 1.3 m (trucks with or without trailers with total two-or three-axle, caravans with a height greater than 1.3 m on the front axle and vans with trailers or caravan with three-axle, buses without a trailer with two or three axles, motor vehicles with a maximum permissible weight which does not exceed 3,500 kg and vehicles registered for over 9 seats).

Vehicles with four or more axles and height measured at the first axis higher than 1.3 m (trucks with or without trailers with four or more axles, tractors with semi-trailers, buses with or without a trailer with four or more axles, vans with trailers or caravan with four or more axles, and motor vehicles with a maximum permissible weight that does not exceed 3.500kg and vehicles registered more than 9 seats).
