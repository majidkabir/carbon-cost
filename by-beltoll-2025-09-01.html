Home News New Tariffs for Tollroads Travel

From September 1, 2025, new tariffs will come into effect in the national electronic toll system BelToll for travel on tollroads of the Republic of Belarus. The relevant changes are fixed in Decree of the Ministry of Transport and Communications of the Republic of Belarus dated August 19, 2025 No. 65 "On Amending the Decree of the Ministry of Transport and Communications of the Republic of Belarus dated June 17, 2013 No. 21”.

For vehicles with the total permissible weight of more than 3.5 tons, the following tariffs will apply from September 1, 2025:

* A calendar year is understood to be a period from January 1 to December 31 of the relevant year, as well as the last 15 calendar days of the previous year and the first 15 calendar days of the following year.

However, vehicle owners who purchased a vignette prior to September 1, 2025 can continue to use it until its expiration date without any additional payments.
