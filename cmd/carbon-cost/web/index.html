What does a truck trip pay for carbon and tolls?

Km-based road tolls by country (with their CO2 part), EU ETS on ferry crossings and ETS2 from 2028. Rates come from the laws (truck toll rates by country); routes and emissions from Sootcase.

Shipments

Each shipment pays its share of the trip by its tonne-km (its weight × the km of its own route from loading to unloading), as EN ISO 14083 and the EU's CountEmissionsEU method do. Legs with nothing on board are empty running, part of the trip.

Emissions stay shared by tonne-km: the GLEC Framework v3.3 takes transport activity as mass × distance.

Many trips at once: a CSV file

One trip a row, with a header naming the fields: from, to, via, date, cargo-kg, mass-kg, axles, euro, co2-class, length-m, l-per-100km, fuel-bought, shipments, country-km, eua, … (the command line's flags). Other columns, such as an order number, are kept. Up to 100 rows; the result is the same file with the costs added. Commas or semicolons between columns; with semicolons, decimal commas and dates such as 03.11.2026 (as Excel writes it where the decimal symbol is a comma). Download an example.
