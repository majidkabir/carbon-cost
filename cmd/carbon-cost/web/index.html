What does a truck trip pay for carbon and tolls?

Km-based road tolls by country (with their CO2 part), EU ETS on ferry crossings and ETS2 from 2028. Rates come from the laws (truck toll rates by country); routes and emissions from Sootcase.

Each shipment pays its share of the trip by its tonne-km (its weight × the km of its own route from loading to unloading), as EN ISO 14083 and the EU's CountEmissionsEU method do. Legs with nothing on board are empty running, part of the trip.
