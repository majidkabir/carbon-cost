Explore our ships

Enjoy a world of comfort, experiences, and great value — on board Color Magic, Color Fantasy, SuperSpeed and Color Hybrid the journey becomes one of the highlights of the trip.

  • SuperSpeed 1 and 2

    The route Hirtshals – Larvik/Kristiansand is operated by SuperSpeed 1 and 2. On board you will find duty free shops, comfortable seats, several dining options and more.

  • Color Magic and Color Fantasy

    The route Kiel – Oslo is operated by Color Magic and Color Fantasy. Experience delicious food, stunning cabins, fitness room and spa, duty free shopping and much more.

  • Color Hybrid

    The route Strömstad - Sandefjord is operated by Color Hybrid. A new standard for greener travel, without compromising on comfort, dining, or onboard experiences.
