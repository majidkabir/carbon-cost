      Our services

      The Strait of Gibraltar is the shortest maritime link between Europe and Africa.

      DFDS operates at least two daily RoRo departures in each direction on the Algeciras–Tanger Med route for unaccompanied freight units. Comprehensive freight handling and transfer solutions are available at both terminals.

      FAQs about Algeciras-Tanger Med (RoRo) route

      • Trailer and reefer trailer
      • ISO container(s), reefer container
      • Mobile machine (with tyres, not chains)
      • Industrial freight
      • Hazardous goods
      • Project cargo

      Dangerous goods are allowed to be shipped depending on the class (Code UN).

      Maximum length: 12 metres

      Maximum width: 2.7 metres

      Maximum weight: 16 tonnes per axle

      Maximum height: 3.9 metres

      • Maximum weight 130t (It's possible to load more, but it all depends on the freight unit, more information will be needed.)
      • Maximum height 5m
