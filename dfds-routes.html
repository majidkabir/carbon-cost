      Why choose DFDS

      Onboard comforts

      Onboard drivers enjoy home-like comforts in first-rate accommodation. Leisure facilities and dining options ensure they are fully rested on arrival.

      Dedicated staff

      DFDS earns its reputation through its people. Thousands of dedicated employees are working hard to make sure millions of lane metres of freight arrive on time, every year.

      With ambitious CO2-reduction targets and significant investment, DFDS has received an environmental award, and its vessels are certified under national, international and EU legislation. Following a 2015 EU ruling, surcharges have been introduced on certain routes.
