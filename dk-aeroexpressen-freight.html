Fare Surcharges Effective June 15, 2026

Rising oil prices and overall cost increases are hitting a small shipping company like ours hard.

We therefore feel compelled to raise our prices slightly in order to balance our budget.

Starting June 15, 2026, a surcharge of 5 kr. will be added to passenger tickets, 10 kr. to passenger cars and vans, and 20 kr. to buses and trucks. We are confident that travelers will understand this measure, as the same rise in costs is also being felt in everyone’s daily lives.

All prices include sales tax plus fuel and service charges. We recommend that you reserve your seat and purchase your ticket online in advance. To reserve a seat and purchase a ticket, click here
