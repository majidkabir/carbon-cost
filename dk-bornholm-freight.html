Important information

Parking of trailers in Køge: On weekdays, trailers may be parked at the trailer carpark until the next departure. Parking of trailers for which carriage on the next departure has not been booked is not allowed. Trailers parked in the trailer carpark, for which no reservation for the next departure has been made, will be moved to a separate area in the carpark to make space for trailers with a reservation. This will result in an additional charge.

Collection of trailers in Køge: Trailers arriving by ferry from Rønne must be collected from the trailer carpark by 19:30 the following day. Trailers that have not been collected before this time will be moved to a separate area in the carpark to make space for other trailers. This will result in an additional charge.

For weekend departures, trailers may be parked in the trailer carpark from Friday. For departures on public holidays, trailers may be parked in the trailer carpark from the last weekday before the relevant public holiday.

A reservation can:

  • Be changed for a fee of DKK 50 until the time of departure
  • Be changed within a month
  • NOT be cancelled or refunded – except for additional purchases such as freight weight, cabin, extra metres etc., which can be refunded by contacting [email protected]

Hazardous goods & Packing certificate

Here, you can download forms for hazardous goods and vehicle packing certificates. Both must be completed before check-in. 

In addition, you can download a list of departures for hazardous goods.

Note that hazardous cargo must be registered no later than:

  • Departure from Køge by 5:00 pm
  • Departure from Rønne by 12:00 pm (noon)

Latest check-in time for vehicles with hazardous goods and LQ is 60 min. before departure, regardless of the vehicle type.
