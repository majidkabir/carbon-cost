Cargo

At Eckerö Line, we are prepared to meet diverse needs and ensure your cargo reaches its destination securely and efficiently.

Eckerö Line provides reliable and high-quality cargo services between Finland and Estonia daily. We have been a trusted partner for cargo services for over 30 years. With two vessels – MS Finlandia and MS Finbo Cargo – we offer a total of 71 departures per week for freight and logistic solutions.

Vessels
Vessels

We offer reliable and high-quality freight services with our two vessels, MS Finbo Cargo and MS Finlandia.

Read more

Our vessels carry a wide variety of goods, including industrial raw materials, components, and food products.

Our vessels carry a wide variety of goods, including industrial raw materials, components, and food products, ensuring smooth supply chains. MS Finbo Cargo operates from Vuosaari in Helsinki to Muuga in Estonia, while MS Finlandia connects Helsinki’s West Harbour to Tallinn’s Old Harbour.

Both vessels MS Finlandia and MS Finbo Cargo are equipped to handle trucks, lorries, vans, oversized vehicles, and dangerous goods (IMO classified items). MS Finbo Cargo, with its wide tailgate and high open deck, is especially suited for transporting over-dimensional units.

Both vessels are equipped to handle trucks, lorries, vans, oversized vehicles, and dangerous goods (IMO classified items). MS Finbo Cargo, with its wide tailgate and high open deck, is especially suited for transporting over-dimensional units such as house modules, boats, large industrial spare parts, and heavy machinery like earthmoving equipment and cranes. We can accommodate vehicles up to 40 meters long, nearly 5 meters wide, and 5 meters high.

With an open deck and a limited number of passengers, MS Finbo Cargo provides the widest range of dangerous goods transportation options on the Finland–Estonia route. The vessel ensures optimal safety and environmental compliance, catering to the unique requirements of your cargo.

Our well-optimized processes guarantee the safe and responsible transport of classified materials, such as chemicals, paint shipments, and electric vehicles. At Eckerö Line, we are prepared to meet diverse needs and ensure your cargo reaches its destination securely and efficiently.

Cargo carbon footprint

Our carbon dioxide emissions have been calculated using the so-called mass method, where the share of passangers and cargo is divided based on weight. Mass method is a common methof for calculating CO2 emissions and is defined in the European standard EN 16258. 

Cargo average CO2-emissions by Eckerö Line 2024 Kg CO2/ Cargo ton / one way
M/S Finlandia (Helsinki, West Harbour T2 – Tallinn, A-terminal) 19,17
M/S Finbo Cargo (Helsinki, Vuosaari – Tallinn, Muuga) 9,89
Cargo average CO2-emissions by Eckerö Line 2025 Kg CO2/ Cargo ton / one way
M/S Finlandia (Helsinki, West Harbour T2 – Tallinn, A-terminal) 17,98
M/S Finbo Cargo (Helsinki, Vuosaari – Tallinn, Muuga) 8,98
