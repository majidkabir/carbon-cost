An authentic archipelago and lighthouse route from Sweden to Åland and Naantali. The new ships Finnsirius and Finncanopus operate on the route two times a day.

Kapellskär–Långnäs–Naantali

Travel comfortably from Sweden to Finland and the Åland Islands with our ships Finnsirius and Finncanopus. The travel time from Kapellskär to Långnäs takes only 3.5 hours and from Kapellskär to Naantali 8–9 hours.

Departures available until 23.12.2027.

Easily get to Stockholm on Tuesdays without your own car

Head to Kapellskär without a car and conveniently continue by bus directly to Stockholm! The bus connection from Kapellskär port to Cityterminalen in Stockholm makes the trip easy and worry-free.

The bus operates on certain Tuesdays between Stockholm’s Cityterminalen and Kapellskär port. From Stockholm to the port the bus runs in the mornings, and from the port to Stockholm in the evenings.
