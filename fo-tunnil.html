You have to pay toll to use the subsea tunnels in the Faroe Islands

Drive through the sub-sea tunnels without stopping. Photo of the license plate will be registered.

There are 3 ways to pay for the trips.

Single Trip

Pay for single trip

Pay for a single return trip before or after driving through a subsea tunnel.

Payment online must be paid no later than 6 days after driving in the tunnel. After that, an invoice will be sent with an additional fee of DKK 50,-

Automatic Payment

Register automatic payment

If you are staying in Faroe Islands for a short period, you can register for automatic payment with your credit card. This will allow your trip expenses to be automatically deducted.

To set up automatic payment, simply register your license plate and the duration for which the payment will be active (e.g., while you're in the Faroe Islands). Your credit card information will be automatically deleted from our system one month after the payment period ends.

If you plan to stay in Faroe Islands for an extended period (such as 10 days or more) or visit regularly, you can sign up for a subscription. This also applies if you live in the Faroe Islands.

Subscription
If you’re spending an extended period in the Faroe Islands or visiting regularly, you can sign up for a subscription. This also applies if you live in the Faroe Islands.
The subscription covers all subsea tunnels and offers a discount. To get the discount, you need to make a prepayment. Please note that there is an annual subscription fee of DKK 200, which will be automatically deducted from your prepayment when you start using the tunnels.

You can subscribe here

With a subscription, do you make an initial prepayment. The rates with a subscription for personel vehicles are DKK 20 for a return trip in Vága- and Norðoyatunnilin, and DKK 75 one way for trips in Eystur- and Sandoyartunnilin. To ensure we have the opportunity to process the refund, we recommend that you make the prepayment a few days before your arrival in the Faroe Islands, and remembering to cancel a few days after your holiday ends. Otherwise, it may not be possible for us to issue a refund.

Rental cars can not obtain a subscription. Toll payment has to be payed to the rental company.

About the sub-sea tunnels

The sub-sea tunnels links a large part of the Faroe Islands together.

Vágatunnilin

Connecting the island of Vágar (Airport) with the island of Streymoy

First blast:        28. september 2000

Price:                 DKK 295 mill. (EUR 40 mill.)

Opened:           10. december 2002

Length:             4.900 meters.

Lowest point: 105 meters below sea level

Financed:         DKK 160,3 mill. (EUR 21 mill) share capital from the Faroese governement. Bankloan DKK 134,7 mill. (EUR 18 mill.) repayed after 15 years.

Vehicles:           In 2023: 1.043.670, average a day 2.859

Security:            According to Norwegian standards

Norðoyatunnilin

Connecting the town of Leirvík with the second largest town Klaksvík

First blast:        28. september 2000

Price:                 DKK 395 mill. ( EUR 53 mill.)

Opened:           29. april 2006

Length:             6.200 meters.

Lowest point: 160 meters below sea level

Financed:         DKK 265,0 mill. (EUR 35 mill.) share capital from the Faroese governement. Bankloan DKK 130,0 mill. (EUR 17 mill) repayed after 15 years.

Vehicles:           In 2023: 1.502.474, average a day 4.116

Security:            According to Norwegian standards

Eysturoyartunnilin

Connecting Tórshavn with the island of Eysturoy

First blast:        21. february 2017

Price:                 DKK 1.300 mill. (EUR 173 mill.)

Opened:           19. december 2020

Length:             Total length 11.240 meters. From Tórshavn to roundabout 7.460 meters, from roundabout to Runavík 2.153 and from the roundabout to Strendur 1.625 meters.

Lowest point: 187 meters below sea level

Financed:         The Eysturoyartunnel and the Sandoyartunnel are owned by the same Ltd. company Eystur- and Sandoyartunlar with a share capital of DKK 400 mill. (EUR 53 mill) from the Faroese Government, and loan at a total of DKK 2.600 mió (EUR 347 mill).

Vehicles:           In 2023: 2.063.169, average a day 5.653

Security:            According to Norwegian standards

Sandoyartunnilin

Connecting the island of Streymoy with the island of Sandoy

First blast:         21. june 2018

Price:                 DKK 1.300 mill. (EUR 173 mill.)

Opened:           20. december 2023

Length:             Total length 10.800 meters.

Lowest point: 155 meters below sea level

Financed:         The Sandoyartunnel and the Eysturoyartunnel are owned by the same Ltd. company Eystur- and Sandoyartunlar with a share capital of DKK 400 mill. (EUR 53 mill) from the Faroese Government, and loan at a total of DKK 2.600 mió (EUR 347 mill).

Vehicles:           Opened in december 2023

Security:            According to Norwegian standards

The sub-sea tunnels are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year and are closed only on rare occasions (usually due to work in the tunnels).

Terms and conditions for P/F Tunnil, P/F Norðoyatunnilin, P/F Vágatunnilin and P/F Eystur- og Sandoyartunlar

1. Services

These terms and conditions are valid for subscriptions that P/F Tunnil, P/F Norðoyatunnilin, P/F Vágatunnilin og P/F Eysturoyar- og Sandoyartunlar offer their customers. The subscriptions for the Vágar, Norðoyggjar, Eysturoy and Sandoy tunnels are now combined in one subscription administered by P/F Tunnil. A subscription gives the subscriber access to the electronic registration system, which registers journeys through all subsea tunnels in the Faroe Islands. The system allows subscribers to drive by the payment stations and through the tunnels with the vehicle registered in the subscription as long as the subscription is valid. In addition to the customer terms and conditions, other special terms and conditions may be set.

2. Parties

The subscription agreement is between P/F Tunnil and the subscriber. Persons of legal age and legal entities can take out subscriptions.

3. Subscriptions are taken out by filling out an application form available on P/F Tunnil webpage.

Data that must be submitted include customer type, name, address, e-mail address and date of birth or V-number as well as information on the vehicle a subscription is taken out for. Information on where bills, messages and other information from P/F Tunnil are to be sent must also be submitted. If required, documentation validating submitted information must be supplied. Subscriptions cannot be taken out if the applicant is in arrears with one of the tunnel companies, P/F Vágatunnilin, P/F Norðoyatunnilin or P/F Eystur- og Sandoyartunlar. A subscriber cannot pass a subscription on to another person.

A subscription with P/F Tunnil is valid on the same terms for all subsea tunnels.

  • To maintain a well-functioning electronic system which ensures that the subscriber can drive past the payment station without stopping.
  • To repair faults in the electronic system as soon as possible.
  • To inform the subscriber when no funds are left in his or her subscription.
  • To describe in detail the services payment is taken for.

Obligations of the subscriber are as follows:

  • To supply P/F Tunnil with correct information when taking out or changing a subscription.
  • To pay in advance for services, which P/F Tunnil, P/F Norðoyatunnilin P/F Vágatunnilin and P/F Eystur- og Sandoyartunlar offer according to terms and conditions for payments. The payment obligation is also valid if other persons have used the service, including unauthorised persons. The payment obligation is not valid if unauthorised usage has been made possible because of negligence on behalf of P/F Tunnil, P/F Norðoyatunnilin, P/F Vágatunnilin and P/F Eystur- og Sandoyartunlar

Announcements on rate hikes are either sent to the subscriber or announced in Faroese media. A decrease in rates can be made without informing the subscriber.

The subscription rates are only valid if the subscriber has made an automatic payment agreement.
