Toll charge and
scheme detail changed
on 1 April 2025

On 1 April 2025, amendments to the toll charges, Penalty Charge Notices and the
Merseyflow Terms & Conditions became effective.

The amendments are detailed below:



1) Toll Charges increased by 20%
Toll charges on the Mersey Gateway and Silver Jubilee bridges increasd by 20% from 1 April 2025. This was
the first increase in seven and a half years since the Mersey Gateway Bridge opened, meaning that the toll
charges increased by less than the cumulative RPI inflation rate which has already reached 40%.

The price of discounted journeys for registered merseyflow customers also increased by similar amounts.

2) Timetable for proposed future changes to toll charges
Future changes to toll charges will be considered once every three (3) years unless there are exceptional
circumstances that require more frequent changes.




3) Penalty Charge Notice and Charge Certificate rates increased
Penalty Charge Notice (“PCN”) rates increased from £40 to £50.

Registered merseyflow customers who pay the PCN within fourteen (14) days will be eligible for a discounted
PCN rate of £25.

From 1 April 2025, there was a new option available to unregistered customers who received a PCN for the
first time. Where an unregistered customer opts to pay the PCN within fourteen (14) days, the unregistered
customer has the option to use the discounted £25 rate to open an account with Merseyflow and have their
PCN cancelled. This will enable the new customer to have discounted crossings in the future.

Charge certificates increased from £60 to £75.

There were no changes to enforcement fees, as these are set by a separate piece of government legislation.
4) Amendments to the Merseyflow Terms and Conditions
The Merseyflow Terms and Conditions cover various vehicle plans, including the Local User Discount Scheme
(“LUDS”) vehicle plan.

From 1 April 2025, the following changes were made to the Merseyflow Terms and Conditions which apply to
the LUDS vehicle plan:

• the registration fee for the LUDS vehicle plan is £12, reflecting a 20% increase;

• the annual renewal of the LUDS vehicle plan is structured as follows:

  • where customers have opened a LUDS vehicle plan prior to 1 April 2025 and have not opted in for
    automatic renewal and set up auto payment, the renewal fee is £12*;

  • where customers have opened a LUDS vehicle plan prior to 1 April 2025 and have opted in for automatic
    renewal and set up auto payment, the annual renewal fee is discounted to £10; and

  • where customers have opened a LUDS vehicle plan on or after 1 April 2025, customers will automatically
    be opted in for the auto-renewal (though they will have an option to opt out) and will also benefit from the
    discounted £10 annual renewal fee once they have set up auto payment.

• customers will be prohibited from registering vehicles with Merseyflow under both a LUDS vehicle plan and
  a Blue Badge vehicle plan. Any customer who qualifies for both discount schemes must choose which plan
  is the most appropriate for them; and

• a fair use policy was introduced to provide greater monitoring rights for Halton Borough Council (including
  where acting through its agent Emovis). Where any LUDS vehicle plan holder makes more than 900
  crossings a year, the LUDS vehicle plan holder may be requested to provide further information to
  demonstrate that the crossings are made in a personal capacity and that the LUDS vehicle plan is not
  being used for business purposes.

*Any Halton resident eligible for the LUDS vehicle plan can sign up for auto renewal/auto payment today by logging into their merseyflow
account and making the switch to auto renewal/auto payment. Please note that in order to qualify for the discounted rate (£10) customers
need to set up auto payment as well as switching to auto renewal.
