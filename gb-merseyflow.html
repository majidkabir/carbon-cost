Toll/Charge

The cost for crossing either the Mersey Gateway Bridge or Silver Jubilee Bridge varies depending on the vehicle you are driving.​

There are various discount options available to help you reduce your journey cost.​

Eligible Halton residents

Can pay a £12 annual admin fee and, once registered, can then make unlimited personal trips a year across either bridge at no extra charge. Annual fee reduced to £10 if you select auto renewal and auto payment.

Blue badge holders

Can pay a £5 one-off registration fee and, once registered, can then make unlimited trips at no extra charge.

Pre-pay account

Anyone, wherever they live, can save up to 10% by paying a £5 one-off fee and registering for a pre-pay sticker account.

Monthly passes​

Anyone who drives a car or a small van can cap their costs by buying an 'unlimited travel' or 'off-peak' monthly pass.

Calculate how much you’ll pay

You can pay for crossings without registering, either by paying in advance or by 11.59pm the day after your journey. If you do this, you will need to pay the full unregistered toll charge for each journey you make.

If you don’t pay on time you will receive a Penalty Charge Notice.
