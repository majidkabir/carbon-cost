Our ships

Ships in Gotland traffic

Destination Gotland has two vessel types in the Gotland service, the newest vessels are powered by liquefied natural gas (LNG) and biogas (LBG) to reduce their climate footprint.


SF1650 - M/S Gotland

M/S Gotland operates the Visby–Nynäshamn/Oskarshamn route.

Key dimensions
  • Built: 2019
  • Length: 199.9 m
  • Beam: 25.86 m
  • Passengers: 1,650
  • Cabin berths: 98
  • Car capacity: 500 cars or 1,745 lane meters
  • Ice class: DNVGL, Ice Class 1A
  • Service speed: 28.5 knots
  • Main machinery: 46,800 kW
