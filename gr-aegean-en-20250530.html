Vehicle Classification

Category 1

Bikes, tricycles (e.g. motorcycles)

Category 2

Light vehicles with or without a trailer and height up to 2.20 m.

Category 3

Vehicles with two or three axles, with or without trailers, and height over 2.20 m.

Category 4

Vehicles with four (4) or more axles, with or without trailers, and height over 2.20 m. (e.g. trucks)
