Shipping Company – Katajanokka Terminal

Home Passengers Passenger Terminals Katajanokka Terminal Shipping Company – Katajanokka Terminal

Katajanokka Terminal has a regular ferry connections: Viking XPRS to Tallinn and MS Gabriella and Viking Cinderella to Stockholm.

During the summer season from 17 June to 9 August 2026, Viking Cinderella and Gabriella will operate alongside Viking XPRS between Helsinki and Tallinn. In addition, the vessels will alternate on Summer Picnic Day Cruises, which call at Tallinn before returning directly to Helsinki without passenger disembarkation.
