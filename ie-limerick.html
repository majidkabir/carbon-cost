Limerick Tunnel

 

Location: N18 Limerick Tunnel. The N18 Limerick Tunnel is located in the Mid-West of Ireland on the outskirts of Limerick City. The road is tolled between Junction 2 and Junction 4.

View the map for the Limerick Tunnel scheme, including toll locations.

TYPE OF VEHICLE APPLICABLE TOLLS 2026
Motorcycles (exceeding 50cc) €1.20
Motor Cars €2.30
Buses or Coaches €4.20
Goods Vehicles with a design gross vehicle weight not exceeding 3,500 kilograms €4.20
Goods Vehicles with a design gross vehicle weight exceeding 3,500 kilograms and having 2 or 3 axles €5.90
Goods Vehicles with a design gross vehicle weight exceeding 3,500 kilograms and having 4 or more axles €7.50
