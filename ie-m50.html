M50 Barrier Free Tolling

 

Location: The M50 motorway is a radial route around Dublin and is intersected by the principal radial routes, i.e., M1, M2, N3, N4 (also serving N6), N7 (also serving N8 & N9) and N11. Barrier-Free Tolling is in operation on the M50 between Junction 6 (M50/N3 Blanchardstown) and Junction 7 (M50/N4 Lucan).

All vehicles using the section of the M50 between Junction 6 (N3 Blanchardstown) and Junction 7 (N4 Lucan) are liable to pay a toll. There is no cash payment facility available at the tolling point on the M50. Unregistered users must pay the toll by 8pm on the day following the journey. Payment can be made by quoting the vehicle registration number:

  • Online at www.eflow.ie
  • By telephone using a Visa / Mastercard credit or Visa Debit card on 0818 50 10 50 / +353 1 461 0122
  • By cash payment at Payzone branded outlets nationwide for further information www.payzone.ie

Unregistered users are required to pay the due toll before 8.00pm on the day after the use of the Toll Road. In accordance with the provisions of the Bye-Laws the Default Tolls for 2025 for late payment are as follows:

  • Initial Default Toll for a user/vehicle class is the Applicable Toll specified for the User/Vehicle Class plus €4.00
  • Additional Default Toll for a user/vehicle class is the Initial Default Toll specified for the User/Vehicle Class plus €51.00
  • Final Default Toll for a user/ vehicle class is the Additional Default Toll specified for the User/Vehicle Class plus €128.00
