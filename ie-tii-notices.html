European Electronic Toll Service (EETS)

European Electronic Toll Service Documents

Directive (EU) 2019/520 aims to streamline electronic road toll systems and facilitate the exchange of information on unpaid road fees across the EU.

In July 2023, this directive was incorporated into Irish law by the Minister of Transport (Statutory Instrument 178/2023).

Below are the documents related to the European Electronic Toll Service (EETS) on TII-operated roads, including the M50 and Dublin Tunnel:

  • Toll domain statement for the Dublin Tunnel
