Tariffs and Passes

List of current tariffs, passes and promotions.

A32 Toll Tariffs (effective from 01/01/2026)

Annually, the rate is adjusted according to the current revision formula, by Decree of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport in consultation with the Ministry of Economy and Finance as established in the Single Agreement between the Concessionaire and the Granting Entity.

 

Class A Vehicles
Avigliana: € 5.50
Salbertrand: € 7.50

Class B Vehicles
Avigliana: € 6.50
Salbertrand: € 8.80

Class 3 Vehicles
Avigliana: € 10.50
Salbertrand: € 14.40

Class 4 Vehicles
Avigliana: € 13.70
Salbertrand: € 18.80

Class 5 Vehicles
Avigliana: € 15.90
Salbertrand: € 21.70

(*) Classification currently in force on the entire Italian motorway network

A: Motorbikes and two-axle vehicles with a height of less than 1.30 m at 1st axle
B: Two-axle vehicles with a height of more than 1.30 m at 1st axle (buses, motor homes, trucks)
3: 3-axle vehicles and tows (buses, three-axle semi-trailer trucks, motor cars with trailer)
4: 4-axle vehicles and tows (motor cars with two-axle trailer or caravan, four-axle trucks and articulated lorries)
5: 5-axle (or more) vehicles and tows (semi-trailer trucks and articulated lorries with 5 or more axles)

Passes (effective from 01/01/2026)

Following the Ministry’s authorisation, SITAF set up monthly toll passes for class A non-commercial vehicles for the exclusive use of residents in the Municipalities of the Mountain Community of the Upper Susa Valley, the Mountain Community of the Susa Valley, the Mountain Community of Olympic Via Lattea Municipalities (belonging to the Susa Valley). In 2022, the discount was also extended to healthcare personnel (medical and paramedical) of Con.ISA, a district of the TO3 Valle di Susa – Val Sangone Local Health Authority, USCA of Oulx, who work in the healthcare facilities located in the Valley.

SITAF Passes are aimed exclusively at individuals and not at companies and are combined with a electronic toll.

The pass is valid every day of the week, with a maximum number of 50 journeys per calendar month, divided into the following types:

Section A: Susa-Bardonecchia, Salbertrand Barrier (maximum no. of journeys/month: 50)

€ 62,10

Section B: Rivoli-Susa, Avigliana Barrier (maximum no. of journeys/month: 50)

€ 49,80

Section C = A + B: Rivoli-Susa and Susa-Bardonecchia, Salbertrand Barrier and Avigliana Barrier (maximum no. of journeys/month: 100, of which 50 at the Avigliana Barrier and 50 at the Salbertrand Barrier)

€ 112,00

To take out a contract, the holder (or person with a proxy) must go to the user Relations Center with all the necessary documents: a electronic toll, identity card, tax code and bank details (IBAN) for the direct debit. Passes cannot be activated if the person is not in possession of one of the aforementioned documents, or if the proxy is not in possession of a valid proxy document.

 

For information and to subscription, please contact the Relations Center

open to the public 9:00 AM-12 PM and 2:00 PM-5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

at Via Torino 127, 10059 Susa (TO)

Telephone at +39 0122 621775

e-mail: centroservizi@sitaf.it

Skipass Promotion 2024-2025

SITAF S.p.A., in cooperation with Colomion S.p.A. and Sestrieres S.p.A., is renewing the “save on the highway and ski in the Susa Valley” promotion for the winter season from 5 December 2025 to 13 April 2026 for A32 highway users who visit the ski resorts of Bardonecchia and the Vialattea.

The promotional rate entitles you to one free transit for every five made, per travel direction and tollgate. It is reserved for users of the A32 Torino–Bardonecchia highway who hold a private-use electronic toll device (NO Business) and pass through the Avigliana and/or Salbertrand tollgates exclusively with Class A vehicles (height at the first axle up to 1.30 m).

You may link one (1) Season Ski Pass card issued by Colomion S.p.A. or Sestrieres S.p.A. to the above device.

To activate the promotion, you must sign the specific contract (forms to be collected from the companies mentioned above). Once completed, it must be sent to the Customer Relations Center office together with the SDD (SEPA Direct Debit), strictly by 30 November 2025.

At the end of the initiative (by the end of April 2026), SITAF S.p.A. will carry out the reconciliation, deducting the transits that qualify as free, and will issue the invoice, which will be sent by email to the address indicated in the contract.

To send the documentation:
email: centroservizi@sitaf.it
or to the Customer Relations Center Offices at Via Torino, 127 — 10059 Susa (TO)
