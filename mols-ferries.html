High-speed ferries

Express 2

Express 2 has set new standards for high-speed ferries in terms of space and comfort. It is easy to drive on and off the large car deck, which is as big as half a football pitch.

When on board, you can enjoy your break in the business lounge, choose from a broad selection of dishes in the restaurant and bistro, or enjoy an exceedingly good cup of coffee in the Espresso House coffee bar.

The Express 2 was built at the Incat shipyard in Tasmania, south of Australia, and only made two stops on the 25,000-kilometre-long journey home to Denmark.
