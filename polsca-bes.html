BES Bunker-Environmental surcharge announcement

  1. We kindly inform you that, effective from November 1, 2026, the Bunker-Environmental Surcharge for PolSca ferry service customers will be as follows:

    • on Świnoujście - Ystad & Świnoujście - Trelleborg link – 13,00 EUR per each commenced linear meter of the vehicle
    • on Gdańsk – Karlshamn link – 16,36 EUR per each commenced linear meter of the vehicle
    • on Gdansk - Nynashamn link – 25,80 EUR per each commenced linear meter of the vehicle
