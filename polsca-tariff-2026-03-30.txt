                                                                                         GENERAL PROVISIONS

 1.    This freight tariff applies to all ferries of the Carrier announced in the official or provisional timetable.


 2.    The cargo ticket will be issued by the Carrier or other authorized entities, as agents, in respect of the goods.

       The shipper is obliged to register the goods for transport clearance at least 2 (two) hours before departure, otherwise the carrier will be entitled to refuse to transport the goods. / Trailers must be ready for
 3.
       loading 4 hours before the scheduled departure of the ferry.

 4.    Rates are expressed in EUR (euro) net and do not include mandatory, official, or statutory fees, charges, customs duties, taxes, etc. Rates are for a one-way journey.

       Rates do not include freight surcharges specified in this tariff. Unless this tariff expressly states otherwise, rates do not include stevedoring costs or other services provided by ports, their agents, or
 5.
       subcontractors for the goods - information on which can be obtained from the Carrier.

 6.    The Carrier reserves the right to change this tariff without prior notice.


 7.    This tariff is prepared in English and Polish, and in the event of any discrepancies between these versions, the English version shall prevail.


 8.    Dangerous goods must be declared for transport at least 24 hours before the scheduled departure of the ferry.



              With the exception of the ferries: MF Mazowia, MF Varsovia, MF Nova Star, MF Wawel, the transport of cargo, including on all types of vehicles, in the form of vehicles
