Scandlines Freight – surcharges April 2026


Dear Customer,



With the current turmoil in Middle East, we experience a sharp increase in the market price for Marine Gasoil (MGO
0.1%), which is the primary reference in our BAF calculation. The BAF mechanism is in place to ensure an ongoing
and transparent adjustment of fuel related costs when the market moves.

Based on the current price development, our BAF will increase as per below:



Rødby-Puttgarden v.v. from 0,82 € per lanemeter in March to 1,15 € per lanemeter in April

Gedser-Rostock v.v. from 2,19 € per lanemeter in March to 3,10 € per lanemeter in April



Timing and what it means for you:

     •     The change will apply in the relevant month in line with our standard BAF practice

     •     If the market prices develops further, the BAF surcharge will be updated in subsequent periods




Detailed information on the surcharges as well as latest traffic information, schedules, tariffs and transport terms are
available at https://freight.scandlines.com at any time.

Please get in touch with your Scandlines sales contact in case of questions or further required information.


Kind regards
