# Romanian, Danish bridge, Hungarian, Baltic, Swedish, Luxembourg, UK, Norwegian, Irish, North Macedonian, Montenegrin, Greek, Turkish motorway and bridge, Fréjus and Mont Blanc tunnel rate tables: how the data is gathered

Everything here runs in the **Tables data** workflow (`.github/workflows/tables-data.yml`, started by pushing to the `tables-data` branch). It fetches the sources, rebuilds the tables, converts them to ORCT datasets, says whether each one is the same as the committed dataset (`tariffs/data`), and pushes the fetched pages, `manifest.json` (URL, sha256, size, fetch time and HTTP status of every file), the rebuilt tables and their datasets to the `tables-out` branch.

1. **Sources** (`sources.json`), fetched by `fetch.py`:
   - **Overpass** (OpenStreetMap queries): `fetch.py` runs them alongside the other sources, on two threads that ask overpass-api.de and overpass.private.coffee first (a query written for another instance asks that one first), then the other instances when one can't be reached, stays busy (429/502/503/504, asked again after 30 and 60 s) or fails the query. A query no instance answers is asked again in a second round after a pause; after three in a row unanswered the rest wait for that round too, and what it can't fetch keeps the last run's copies.
   - **Romania**: CNAIR's eToll TollRo page. Its tariff widget reads the pricing offer in force from the page's portlet (`rest_path=/pricing-offers/active-pricing-offer`, `productClass=PER_KM`); `fetch.py` finds the portlet's resource URL in the page and saves that JSON.
   - **Denmark**: A/S Storebælt's business price page and Øresundsbron's truck price page.
   - **Fréjus tunnel**: SFTRF's price list for the French platform (PDF, converted with `pdftotext -layout`).
   - **Mont Blanc tunnel**: ATMB's price page for heavy vehicles.
   - **Latvia**: likumi.lv's consolidated Autoceļu lietošanas nodevas likums (Annex 2 is in the page).
   - **Estonia**: the Liiklusseadus in force from Riigi Teataja's public API, which its pages are built from: `/public-api/api/v1/akt/113032019015?leiaKehtiv=true` names the version in force (`kehtivId`), whose text is `/public-api/api/v1/akt/<kehtivId>/blob-html`. The same for the ferry price regulation (nr 120); an act's annexes are the PDFs its text links (`<id>-lisa-<n>.pdf`).
   - **Lithuania**: e-TAR and e-seimas refuse scripted requests (Cloudflare, status 403; `sources.json` keeps the URLs so the manifest shows it). The consolidated resolution No 447 was opened in Chrome instead: its page's text is in a frame (`/rs/actualedition/TAR.479B13E87700/<edition code>/`), whose text was read and saved, from the annex heading to point 6, as `browser/lt-447.txt` with the URL, date and edition in its header.
   - **Transport for London and RIS**: both refuse the workflow (and the Internet Archive's copies are gone or unreachable). Their pages were opened in Chrome and the needed text saved, each file with its URL and the day it was read in its header: `browser/gb-tfl-lez-pay.txt` (LEZ daily charges, with the Mayor's notice of the Euro VI standard), `browser/gb-tfl-tunnels.txt` (Blackwall and Silvertown tunnels: TfL's payment page, vehicle classes and the press release with the charges before and from 21 September 2026), `browser/at-nehg-10.txt` (NEHG 2022 § 10 (1), consolidated). Read again in Chrome when the charges change.
   - **When to read them again**: `check_browser.py` knows each capture's yearly change day or review cycle (e.g. the Warnowtunnel's Anlage each autumn, the M6 Toll quarterly, Greek and Liefkenshoek tolls each January) and warns from its header's read date; the weekly *Pages read in Chrome* workflow fails when one is due, so GitHub mails the owner.
   - **TT-Line**: the site refuses scripted requests (status 403, the timetable PDFs aside, which don't name ships). Its freight timetable was opened in Chrome and each route searched (Travemünde, Rostock and Świnoujście to Trelleborg, for 19, 22 and 25 November 2026); the departures' text and the ships' initials as the page names them are in `browser/ttline-timetable.txt`, which `ferry_ships.py` reads. Read again when TT-Line's ships change routes.
   - **NV Tunnel Liefkenshoek**: a bot check refuses the workflow; the tariffs page and the FAQ's category answer were opened in Chrome and saved as `browser/be-liefkenshoek.txt` (each year's table as shown). Read again when the next year's tariffs appear.
   - **Greece**: `gr.py` reads Egnatia Odos and Attiki Odos from fetch.py's `gr-egnatia.html` and `gr-attiki.html`; if those aren't fetched or carry no prices, from `browser/gr-egnatia.txt` and `browser/gr-attiki.txt` (the pages read in Chrome; current year only). Read again when the prices change (each January).
   - **M6 Toll**: the pricing page's zone map is drawn by its script, so the workflow's copy lacks it; the map was opened in Chrome and each junction's zone and entry/exit by direction saved as `browser/gb-m6toll-zones.txt`. `m6toll.py` reads it with fetch.py's `gb-m6toll.html`, `gb-m6toll-tables.html` and `gb-m6toll-osm.json`.
   - **Landesrecht Mecklenburg-Vorpommern**: the portal is a script-driven app and the Warnowtunnel's own site has a certificate for another name; the Warnow-QuerungMautHVO M-V's Gesamtausgabe was opened in Chrome and its text saved as `browser/de-warnow-mauthvo.txt` (the Anlage as a `|` table). Read again when the change history lists a new Anlage (each autumn).
   - **Sweden**: Riksdagen's open-data text of Lag (1997:1137) and the PDF of SFS 2025:918 (the year's amounts in kronor, converted with `pdftotext -layout`).
   - **Luxembourg**: Legilux's file of the consolidated RGD du 21 mai 2019 (the page `…/a353/consolide/20241224` loads it from `/filestore/…-fr-html.html`).
   - **UK**: GOV.UK's guidance on the HGV levy service, which tabulates the levy's rates in force. legislation.gov.uk's own pages of the Act can't be kept on `tables-out`: GitHub's secret scanning rejects a key-like string in them.
   - **Norway**: the NVDB API's toll stations (object type 45, with properties, location, geometry and version dates; `fetch.py` sends an `X-Client` header, which the API asks for) and the road link sequences they are placed on (`/vegnett/api/v4/veglenkesekvenser`, 50 at a time).
   - **Ireland**: TII's page for each toll road ("Toll Locations and Charges"), and the toll booths and main roads of Geofabrik's Ireland and Northern Ireland OpenStreetMap extract (filtered with `osmium`; its date saved as `ie-osm-timestamp.txt`).
   - **Swedish bridges**: Riksdagen's text of Förordning (2014:1564), Transportstyrelsen's page for each bridge, and the toll gantries named after the bridges in Geofabrik's Sweden OpenStreetMap extract.
   - **North Macedonia**: the PDFs of the toll decision (Службен весник 118/2019, for the categories) and its 2025 amendment, whose toll table is an image: transcribed by hand into `transcribed/mk-2025.json` with the PDF's sha256, which `check_transcribed.py` compares with the fetched PDF; and the toll booths ("Патарина <station>") of Geofabrik's North Macedonia OpenStreetMap extract.
   - **Montenegro**: Monteput's price pages for the motorway and the Sozina tunnel (when Monteput answers the workflow with a bot check, `me.py` reads the copies read in Chrome, `browser/me-autoput.txt` and `browser/me-sozina.txt`, and the sources say so), and the toll booths and motorway junctions of Geofabrik's Montenegro OpenStreetMap extract.
   - **Greece**: the toll price pages of Olympia Odos, Nea Odos, Kentriki Odos, Moreas and Aegean Motorway and Gefyra's Rio–Antirrio bridge prices, and the toll booths and motorway ways of Geofabrik's Greece OpenStreetMap extract. (`fetch.py` gunzips a response a server sends compressed unasked.)
   - **Swedish congestion tax**: Lag (2004:629) om trängselskatt from the Riksdag's open data (the .text form), and Transportstyrelsen's toll gantries in Geofabrik's Sweden extract.
   - **CalMac**: the commercial vehicle rates page (and the Internet Archive's latest copy of it, as the page refuses the workflow at times) and its fares table (an image), a route's fare page, and the ferry lines, terminals and a few places on Scotland's west coast (Overpass).
   - **Jadrolinija**: each state ferry line's timetable and price list for the year (PDF) and the line's page that links it, and the ferry lines, terminals and a few places on the Croatian coast (Overpass).
   - **Strait of Messina**: Caronte & Tourist's commercial vehicle fares page (monthly) and the ferry line and terminals (Overpass).
   - **Bornholmslinjen**: the freight prices page (each year it lists) and the ferry lines, terminals and places around Bornholm (Overpass).
   - **Danish domestic ferries** (`dk_ferries.py`): the freight pages of SAMSØLINJEN, ALSLINJEN, FANØLINJEN, LANGELANDSLINJEN, MOLSLINJEN and Samsø Rederi (truck prices by length; each year a page lists), and the price pages of Ærøfærgerne and ÆrøXpressen, and Denmark's ferry lines and terminals (Overpass).
   - **Scandlines** (`scandlines.py`): the freight tariffs page and the PDFs it links (Germany–Denmark from 10 m, small units, transit to Sweden, Øresundslinjen's Helsingør–Helsingborg), the surcharges page and each month's notice it links (January to May 2026 from the Internet Archive's copies), and the ferry lines and terminals of the western Baltic and the Øresund (Overpass).
   - **POLSCA Baltic Ferries** (`polsca.py`): Polferries' and Unity Line's joint freight tariff page on unityline.eu and the edition it links, the 30 March 2026 edition, the BES and ETS pages and the announcements they link (fetch.py saves each linked PDF as `<id>-<file name>.pdf`), and the ferry lines and terminals between Poland and Sweden (Overpass). A version per tariff edition or BES announcement.
   - **Elbe ferry Glückstadt–Wischhafen** (`de_elbfaehre.py`): FRS Elbfähre's fares for trucks and cargo (German page; 2025 and the 2026 fares before November's change from the Internet Archive's copies), its fuel surcharge page (earlier states from the Internet Archive), and the ferry line and terminals (Overpass). The 2026 fares the live page gives are taken from the day first read (19 November 2026); the builder stops if the page gives others again.
   - **ETS island derogations** (`ets_islands.py` → `out/ferries/crossings-islands.json`, compared with `ferries/data/crossings-islands.json`): Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2895's Annex I (the Official Journal text, from the Publications Office) and Jadrolinija's lines: every pair of ports on a line with a Croatian island port the Decision lists is a crossing with no ETS until 2030.
   - **Ferry crossings' ships** (`ferry_ships.py` → `out/ferries/crossings-ships.json`, compared with `ferries/data/crossings-ships.json`): POLSCA's timetables (the timetables page on unityline.eu and every timetable it links: a ship with a departure sails the crossing; one the page says is out of service is left out), Bornholmslinjen's ferries page (each ferry's crossings), TT-Line's freight timetable (`browser/ttline-timetable.txt`), Stena Line Freight's route pages (each route's vessels; fetch.py follows the route list's links, a source's `follow` pattern) and Finnlines' route pages and master schedule (the ferries on each route), with the terminals on OpenStreetMap's ferry lines named after both ports (`ferry-ships-osm`, `ferry-ships-osm-2`; `python3 ferry_ships.py --osm-query` prints the queries from its port table; each port at the line's end nearest its town); and, for the ports outside the EU and the EEA in that table (the UK, the Isle of Man, the Channel Islands, the Faroes; Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Albania, Montenegro, Turkey), every OpenStreetMap ferry line to one of them, as a crossing whose ships aren't named but whose ports' countries give its share of the EU ETS, with the terminals on OpenStreetMap's ferry lines and the IMO numbers from the EU MRV reports' ship lists; Køge–Rønne gets the island derogation the Decision gives Bornholm's Rønne.
   - **Serbia**: the putarina decision's amendment in force (Službeni glasnik 57/2026, `rs-sg-57-2026.pdf` from pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs), whose tables are scans: `tools/rs/gazette_ocr.py` reads categories III and IV (Tesseract's Serbian model; each price from both mirror cells, IV checked against III) into `rs-sg-57-2026.json`, kept from the last run while the issue's content is the same; Putevi Srbije's price list (`rs-cene.pdf`), whose RSD amounts the table is held to; and the toll booths of Geofabrik's Serbia extract.
   - **Serbia (Belgrade bypass)**: Putevi Srbije's bypass price list (`cene_putarina_obilaznica_lat.pdf`), and the motorways and motorway junctions of Geofabrik's Serbia extract by Belgrade.
   - **Portugal**: IMT's "Taxas de portagem" page, and the motorways and motorway junctions of Geofabrik's Portugal extract.
   - **Spain (SEITT)**: SEITT's 2026 tariff PDFs for AP-36, AP-41 and AP-7 Cartagena–Vera, and the toll booths, motorways and links of Geofabrik's Madrid, Castilla-La Mancha, Murcia and Andalucía extracts.
   - **Turkey**: KGM's 2026 bridge and motorway tariffs (PDFs linked from its "Otoyol ve Köprü Geçiş Ücretleri" page; the workflow also writes each one's word boxes with `pdftotext -bbox`), and Geofabrik's Turkey OpenStreetMap extract: the ways named after the bridges, and the toll booths, gantries and motorway junctions.
   - **Hungary**: the Nemzeti Jogszabálytár's consolidated text of 25/2013. (V. 31.) NFM rendelet. The site sends long parts (the annexes) separately; `fetch.py` asks for them as its page script does (`/ajax/njtGetBlock.json`).
2. **Build**:
   - `ro.py` → `ro-<date>.json`: lei/km including 21% VAT by mass category, road category and emission class, from the offer JSON; the date and order number from the offer's name.
   - `dk_bridges.py` → `dk-bridges-<year>-01-01.json`: Storebælt's express prices for trucks by length (including 25% VAT) and its green discount; Øresund's normal and ØresundBUSINESS prices (excluding VAT).
   - `frejus.py` → `frejus-<date>.json`: one-way class 3 and 4 prices for trucks (EURO 5-6), a crossing priced at the French plaza.
   - `mont_blanc.py` → `mont-blanc-<date>.json`: one-way class 3 and 4 prices (France to Italy; Italy to France is the same before VAT), a crossing priced at the French plaza.
   - `it.py` → `it-<date>.json`: Autostrade per l'Italia's plain and mountain unit rates (VAT included) and their date from its tariff page, as the default motorway rate; one row per concession and class from the 2026 unit rates for users on the pages of SATAP, Ivrea Torino Piacenza (A21, A55/A5), Asti-Cuneo and SAV and in the A22's tariff PDF (`it-satap-a4`, `it-itp-a21`, `it-itp-a5`, `it-asti-cuneo`, `it-sav`, `it-a22`), CAV's 2026 rates from its image as transcribed in `transcribed/it-cav-2026.json` (`it-cav-rates`; a page whose rates aren't for the table's year is left out), and for the other concessions from `transcribed/it-mit-*.json` (the 2025 unit rates in the concessions' sheets the Ministry published, transcribed by hand from the PDFs; `check_transcribed.py` says if a PDF changed), unit rate plus ANAS surcharge times 1.22 VAT, keyed by the OpenStreetMap operator (and ref) the roads data records for Italy's toll motorways.
   - `it_cas.py` → `it-cas-<date>.json`: the Consorzio per le Autostrade Siciliane's A18 and A20 tolls by entry and exit station from its PDFs (`it-cas-a18`, `it-cas-a20`), as two closed systems of stretches placed from OSM (`it-cas-a18-links-osm`, `it-cas-a20-links-osm`; signposted junctions, the terminal barriers at toll-booth clusters; carriageway pieces OSM breaks are joined first). `it.py` gives the Consorzio's ways a rate of 0 so they aren't priced twice.
   - `it_barriers.py` → `it-barriers-<date>.json`: Milan's ring-road barriers (Terrazzano, Vimercate, Sesto San Giovanni) as toll points, their latest year's tolls to the user from Milano Serravalle's PDFs (`it-mise-terrazzano`, `it-mise-vimercate`, `it-mise-sesto`), placed at their toll booths in OSM (`it-barriers-osm`; Vimercate's unnamed booths as those within 1 km of the Vimercate Sud junction). `it.py` gives the ring roads' ways a rate of 0.
   - `lv.py` → `lv-<date>.json`: Annex 2's 1-day rates by mass, axles and EURO class (zero-emission as CO2 class 5), a `per_day` table; the date from the annex heading.
   - `ee.py` → `ee-<date>.json`: § 190^3's 24-hour rates, each paragraph read for its mass, axles and EURO class (zero-emission as CO2 class 5); valid from the latest entry-into-force date in § 190^3.
   - `ee_ferries.py` → `ee-ferries-<date>.json`: regulation nr 120 in force (Riigi Teataja's API, its text and annex `ee-ferries-lisa-1.txt`): the truck ticket per line, × 1.2 on Fridays from 13:00 towards the islands and on Sundays from 13:00 back (§ 6); ports on OpenStreetMap's car ferry lines (`ee-ferries-osm.json`).
   - `lt.py` → `lt-<date>.json` from `browser/lt-447.txt`: point 1's daily rates for N2 and N3 by the motor vehicle's axles and EURO class, a `per_day` and `motor_axles` table; dates from the edition line; point 6's roads.
   - `se.py` → `se-<year>-01-01.json`: the förordning's daily amounts (the last number of each row) by axles, CO2 class and EURO class.
   - `lu.py` → `lu-<date>.json`: art. 1er 2°'s daily amounts ("Pour une journée") by CO2 class, EURO class and axles; the date from "À compter du"; the annex's roads.
   - `gb.py` → `gb-<date>.json`: the daily rates by weight band, Euro 6 or older; zero-emission exempt; the date from "Levy rates from".
   - `no.py` → `no-<date>.json`: one toll point per AutoPASS station with its large-vehicle price, rush-hour price and rush hours, charged heading (the metering direction of its road link at the station, turned round for "Mot metrering") and hour-rule group; dated by the stations' latest version date. It prints the stations left out and why. The rush hours, rush-free days and what the hour rule charges come from each project's tariff decision (`RUSH` in `no.py`, sources `no-rush-*`): the script checks the station's NVDB rush hours against them and warns where a fetched source no longer says what they were taken from.
   - `no_ferries.py` → `no-ferries-<date>.json`: Norway's national road ferries as closed systems, one per route, from the AutoPASS ferry price table (zone × length group AP1-AP9, VAT taken out) and regulativ, the Minister of Transport's zones by stretch (checked against the table: the 2025 AP1 price is the 2026 one less 3.6%), NVDB's routes (770) and quays (64), and OpenStreetMap's car ferry lines. Each quay's points lie out on the water on its car ferry lines to the route's other quays (0.5 km out, further where another ferry line runs within 0.3 km), matched within 0.15 km. Moss–Horten from its own price list (the operator's fares for the year by length; the 10% and 30% columns checked). It stops if a source no longer says what it was read from.
   - `ie.py` → `ie-<year>-01-01.json`: one toll point per plaza with its heavy-vehicle prices; each plaza is the OSM toll booths on its road's main carriageway (ramp booths left out), the road given per page in the script.
   - `se_bridges.py` → `se-bridges-<date>.json`: one toll point per bridge (its gantries' centre) with the heavy-truck amount; valid from Förordning (2024:194)'s entry into force.
   - `mk.py` → `mk-<date>.json`: one toll point per booth with the category III and IV euro prices; a booth on a one-way carriageway charges its direction, booths charging both ways are merged.
   - `me.py` → `me-2022-07-21.json`: the motorway's entry–exit prices (the calculator's `items` in the page) as a closed toll system, and the Sozina tunnel as a toll point; stations are the booths named "Naplatna stanica <name>", else the booths within 1 km of the junction of that name.
   - `gr.py` → `gr-2026-01-01.json`: one toll point per booth (a station's two-way booths merged) with the category 3 and 4 prices divided by 1.24; page names matched to the OSM booths ("Μετωπικά/Πλευρικά Διόδια <name>") without accents, nominatives put in the genitive by a table in the script.
   - `se_congestion.py` → `se-congestion-{stockholm,goteborg}-<date>.json`: the amount schedules of bilaga 1 (inner city, Essingeleden; low and high season) and bilaga 2, the caps, and the rules on eves, July and Göteborg's 60 minutes, each checked against the law's wording; Transportstyrelsen's gantries from OSM as toll points by schedule.
   - `pt.py` → `pt-<year>-01-01.json`: IMT's lanços laid along each motorway's longest carriageway in OSM by their lengths (ends at junction nodes, best fit either way; interchange ends checked against the other motorway), a toll point in each lanço's middle with its class 2–4 rates divided by 1.23; the Lusoponte bridges northbound at their main span, by 1.06.
   - `es_seitt.py` → `es-seitt-<year>-01-01.json`: each SEITT PDF's three matrices read from word boxes (Telepeaje and Efectivo sub-columns), Pesados 1 and 2 cash prices and tag prices divided by 1.21, free 00:00–05:59; stations placed at OSM toll booth clusters whose nearby links are signposted to them (one candidate each, by elimination, checked to lie between their neighbours).
   - `es_ministry.py` → `es-ap66-<date>.json`, `es-ap46-<date>.json`, `es-ap7ac-<date>.json` from the Ministry of Transport's tables of the tolls in force (`es-min-<motorway>-<year>.txt`, on its CDN, 2025 and 2026): AP-66 as a closed system by pair of stations, placed at the toll plazas and accesses of Aucalsa's own interactive map (its Google My Maps as KML, `es-map-aucalsa.html`); AP-46 and AP-7 Alicante–Cartagena as barriers with low and high season prices (`high_rate`, `high_season`), placed at OpenStreetMap's toll plazas (`es-min-<motorway>-osm.json`). AP-9, AP-53 and AP-68 are fetched but not built: their stations can't yet be placed from an official map.
   - `m6toll.py` → `gb-m6toll-<date>.json`: the M6 Toll as a closed system priced by zones (see the M6 Toll note above); stations are the middle of each carriageway's stretch between junctions, with the carriageway's heading, matched within 0.1 km.
   - `ba.py` → `ba-<date>.json`, one table per act: the FBiH Government's decrees SN 86/20 (`ba-uredba-86-20.txt`) and SN 63/24 (`ba-uredba-63-24.txt`, the Official Gazette issue as the FBiH legal aid office publishes it) and JP Autoceste FBiH's price list from 1 November 2026 (`ba-cjenovnik-2026-11.txt`): per direction, a direction and its reverse as one directed closed system, single mainline barriers (Svilaj) as toll points; stations at JP Autoceste FBiH's OSM toll booths (`ba-osm.json`), those without one left out and named in the law; KM converted at the fixed 1.95583 per euro.
   - `ba_rs.py` → `ba-rs-<date>.json` from `transcribed/ba-rs-<station>.json` (Autoputevi Republike Srpske's price list at each of its 9 toll stations, published only as images; fetch.py's `rs-ba-cj-<station>.pdf` give the sha256 `check_transcribed.py` compares) and the operator's OSM toll booths (`rs-ba-osm.json`): one closed system by entry and exit station.
   - `es_basque.py` → `es-basque-2026-01-01.json`: Gipuzkoa's N-I/A-15 canon (Bidegi's 2026 matrix, `es-bidegi-n1-a15-2026.bbox.html` word boxes: Pesados 1 and 2 by entry row and exit column, with each station's road and PK) as one directed closed system, prices divided by 1.21; Norma Foral 5/2021's classes, IVA and EURO percentages checked against `es-gipuzkoa-nf-5-2021.txt`. Each road's PK is fitted to its OSM carriageways (`es-basque-osm.json`) from the junctions' exit numbers, a station placed at its PK (or at its junction where the exit number is its alone); a station of the system is each carriageway's stretch between two consecutive entries/exits, at its middle with its heading, stretches joined across the pieces OSM splits the roads into where they meet.
   - `es_stretch.py`: shared by the next four: carriageways from OSM ways (forks followed by the least turn), junctions with the words their exit links are signposted with, the longest order-keeping match of a tariff's stations to them, and the stretches between consecutive stations as a closed system.
   - `es_ap53.py` → `es-ap53-<year>-01-01.json`: the Ministry's AP-53 pairs (`es-min-ap53-<year>.txt`) on the stretches of `es-ap53-links-osm.json`; Ribadulla and Silleda pinned by their named toll booths, Alto de Santo Domingo at the motorway's end.
   - `es_radials.py` → `es-radials-<year>-01-01.json`: SEITT's R-2, R-3, R-4 and R-5 matrices (read by `es_seitt.read`) on the stretches of `es-radials-links-osm.json`; each station placed by the place names and road refs in its name; the motorway's own ends for a first or last station no exit is signposted to.
   - `es_basque_ap8.py` → `es-basque-ap8-2026-01-01.json`: Bidegi's AP-8/AP-1 sheet (`es-bidegi-ap8-2026.bbox.html`: the first matrix's 17 stations by three sub-rows, columns by the prices' right edges; the Donostia – Behobia matrix) as two closed systems on the stretches of `es-basque-osm.json` + `es-basque-links-osm.json`; plazas without an exit placed at the carriageway's toll booth clusters (three or more within 200 m).
   - `es_ap68.py` → `es-ap68-<year>-01-01.json`: the Ministry's AP-68 tables (`es-min-ap68-<year>.txt`: the general tariff, Haro–Alfaro and Zambrana–Tudela with tag) on the stretches of `es-ap68-links-osm.json`; stations no exit is signposted to placed by elimination (`es_stretch.fill`).
   - `tr.py` → `tr-<date>.json`: a toll point per carriageway at the middle of each bridge's main span (prices from the pdftotext lines "<class> … <price>"), and a closed system per motorway from its matrix (`tr_matrix.py` reads the word boxes: class rows, price columns, the header words over each column); stations placed at the OSM booths of their name (else the junction), the shortest chain where a name matches several, each booth serving one motorway; prices divided by 1.20. The 1915 Çanakkale bridge's tariff is checked against Malkara–Çanakkale's Gelibolu Güney – bridge price.
   - `hu.py` → `hu-<date>.json` and `tools/roads/hu-annex1.json`: § 1 (2)-(4a) (infrastructure charge), annex 1 (tolled sections, with each one's suburban share) and annex 2 (external-cost charge).
   - `is_km.py` → `is-<year>-01-01.json` (2026, 2027, 2028 to 2029, from 2030): Lög nr. 100/2025's kílómetragjald from `is-althingi-100-2025.html` (the Internet Archive's copy of lagasafn 157b): 6. gr.'s bands by gjaldþyngd, the annex's maxima by axles from 10,000 kg, temporary provisions VI and VII (the trailer at 60 % and 80 %, trailers from 10,000 kg in 2026, clean-energy trucks at 20 %); rows per vehicle (`vehicle_unit` motor and trailer, `energy`). The 2024 and 2025 tables (Lög nr. 87/2004) are built by `tools/history/build_is.py`.
   - `by.py` → `by-<date>.json`: BelToll's euro per km by axles (2, 3, 4+) from Mintrans resolution No. 42 on ETALON-online (`by-mintrans-42-2026.html`, else `browser/by-mintrans-42-2026.txt`), as "motorway" rows on the ways `tools/roads` keeps as Belarus's toll roads; earlier tariffs by `tools/history/build_by.py` from BelToll's announcements.
   - `gb_calmac.py` → `gb-calmac-<date>.json`: CalMac's commercial vehicle single rates (`gb-calmac-cv.pdf`, an image: read by OCR, each route's base and price per 0.5 m checked against its 23 printed fares; the rates page `gb-calmac-cv-page.html` must link it), by length in 0.5 m (the fares exclude VAT: `gb-calmac-uig-tarbert.pdf`), as closed systems whose ports are stations out on OpenStreetMap's car ferry lines (`gb-calmac-osm.json`); routes whose lines share a way or run together from a port are one system.
   - `hr_jadrolinija.py` → `hr-jadrolinija-<year>-01-01.json`: Jadrolinija's state ferry lines (`hr-jadro-<line>.txt`, each line's page `hr-jadro-<line>-page.html` must link it): goods vehicles by load capacity, flat fares up to 3, 4 and 5 t and a fare per tonne over 5 t (ORCT unit `t` on `load_capacity_kg`), off-season and season, VAT (25%) taken out; ports on OpenStreetMap's car ferry lines (`hr-jadrolinija-osm.json`, `ferry_osm.py`: where no one line joins two ports, the shortest way along lines that meet).
   - `it_messina.py` → `it-messina-<month's first day>.json`: one way each direction by length band (to 20.50 m) plus the port company's 4.30 EUR per trip; directed, on OpenStreetMap's ferry line Tremestieri–Villa San Giovanni.
   - `dk_bornholm.py` → `dk-bornholm-<year>-01-01.json`, one per year the page prices: loaded prices on Rønne–Køge, Rønne–Ystad and Rønne–Sassnitz by the truck's length (lorry to 10 and 12 m, semi-trailer to 17 m, road train with each metre over 19 m); on OpenStreetMap's ferry lines.
   - `rs.py` → `rs-<date>.json`: every station pair of each section of the decision's tables (`tools/rs/gazette.py`), in RSD, at the OSM toll booths of each station's name; it stops when the price list is one from another day (a new amendment) or its RSD amounts differ.
   - `rs_bypass.py` → `rs-bypass-<date>.json`: the Belgrade bypass's category 4 toll per section (Putevi Srbije's `rs-obilaznica.pdf`, RSD) as toll points with a heading, one in the middle of each carriageway between the interchanges' junctions (the shortest way along OpenStreetMap's A1 in `rs-bypass-osm.geojsonseq`, cut from Geofabrik's Serbia extract by Belgrade); a crossings table, on top of the motorway toll.
   - `pl_a4.py` → `pl-a4-<date>.json`: the A4 Katowice-Kraków (Stalexport Autostrada Małopolska) price list from `pl-a4-tolls.html` (else `browser/pl-a4-cennik.txt`): toll points at the Mysłowice (Brzęczkowice) and Balice plazas, classes by the motor vehicle's axles and the trailer (`motor_axles`, `trailer`).
   - `pl_a1.py` → `pl-a1-<date>.json`: the A1 Rusocin-Toruń (AmberOne) closed system from `pl-a1-tolls.html`'s table for categories 2-4, stations at their junctions and booths (`pl_osm.py`, `pl-concessions-osm.json`).
   - `pl_a2.py` → `pl-a2-<date>.json`: the A2 Świecko-Konin (Autostrada Wielkopolska) station-to-station prices from `pl-a2-cat2/3/4.txt` (pdftotext of the category PDFs) and the date on `pl-a2-tolls.html`; groups by the motor vehicle's axles and the trailer. Stations free to a neighbour with the same prices (the Poznań ring, Świecko) are left out when OpenStreetMap has no point of them. `tools/roads` leaves these three concession stretches out of Poland's e-TOLL roads.
   - `fuel_co2.py` → `fuelco2-<cc>-<date>.json`: national CO2 prices on fuel, per tonne of CO2 (DE, AT, IE, PT, SI) or per litre of diesel (DK, SE, NO, FI, LT, LU, IS, and Poland's opłata emisyjna from art. 321f of the Prawo ochrony środowiska's consolidated texts; Iceland's kolefnisgjald from three versions of Lög nr. 129/2009 in Alþingi's lagasafn, the Internet Archive's copies).
   - `tunnels.py` → tunnels and crossings priced per passage, among them Envalira (`ad-envalira-*`), the Faroe Islands' subsea tunnels (`fo-tunnels-*`, Tunnil's heavy-vehicle prices, one-way where charged one way), Vaðlaheiðargöng (`is-vadlaheidargong-*`) and the Ring Road through Hornafjörður (`is-hornafjordur-*`, Spölur's prices checked against Vegagerðin's notice), each placed from its own Overpass query.
3. **Transcribed images**: Atlandes (A63) and ALBEA (A150) publish their prices only as images. Those are transcribed by hand into `tools/fr/transcribed/*.json`, each with the image's URL and sha256; the workflow fetches the images (`sources.json`) and `check_transcribed.py` says whether each is still the one transcribed.
4. **Convert**: `tools/orct/convert.py` turns the rebuilt tables into ORCT datasets right away (`out/orct`); only those are kept.
5. **Compare**: the workflow prints, for each dataset, whether it is the same as the committed one in `tariffs/data`, or the difference. A new dataset means a new price list: copy it into `tariffs/data/<cc>/` (a table with an end date on the previous version if they overlap, converted again) and run `tools/orct/convert.py --index tariffs/data`.

The Danish plazas' coordinates are fixed in `dk_bridges.py` (OpenStreetMap toll booths). The Hungarian EUR/HUF rate (388.60, 209/2013. Korm. rendelet § 3/A) and the HU-GO checks are in `hu.py`.
