# Romanian, Danish bridge, Hungarian, Baltic, Swedish, Luxembourg, UK, Norwegian, Irish, North Macedonian, Montenegrin, Greek, Turkish motorway and bridge, Fréjus and Mont Blanc tunnel rate tables: how the data is gathered

Everything here runs in the **Tables data** workflow (`.github/workflows/tables-data.yml`, started by pushing to the `tables-data` branch). It fetches the sources, rebuilds the tables, says whether each one is the same as the committed file, and pushes the fetched pages, `manifest.json` (URL, sha256, size, fetch time and HTTP status of every file) and the rebuilt tables to the `tables-out` branch.

1. **Sources** (`sources.json`), fetched by `fetch.py`:
   - **Romania**: CNAIR's eToll TollRo page. Its tariff widget reads the pricing offer in force from the page's portlet (`rest_path=/pricing-offers/active-pricing-offer`, `productClass=PER_KM`); `fetch.py` finds the portlet's resource URL in the page and saves that JSON.
   - **Denmark**: A/S Storebælt's business price page and Øresundsbron's truck price page.
   - **Fréjus tunnel**: SFTRF's price list for the French platform (PDF, converted with `pdftotext -layout`).
   - **Mont Blanc tunnel**: ATMB's price page for heavy vehicles.
   - **Latvia**: likumi.lv's consolidated Autoceļu lietošanas nodevas likums (Annex 2 is in the page).
   - **Estonia**: the Liiklusseadus in force from Riigi Teataja's public API, which its pages are built from: `/public-api/api/v1/akt/113032019015?leiaKehtiv=true` names the version in force (`kehtivId`), whose text is `/public-api/api/v1/akt/<kehtivId>/blob-html`.
   - **Lithuania**: e-TAR and e-seimas refuse scripted requests (Cloudflare, status 403; `sources.json` keeps the URLs so the manifest shows it). The consolidated resolution No 447 was opened in Chrome instead: its page's text is in a frame (`/rs/actualedition/TAR.479B13E87700/<edition code>/`), whose text was read and saved, from the annex heading to point 6, as `browser/lt-447.txt` with the URL, date and edition in its header.
   - **Transport for London and RIS**: both refuse the workflow (and the Internet Archive's copies are gone or unreachable). Their pages were opened in Chrome and the needed text saved, each file with its URL and the day it was read in its header: `browser/gb-tfl-lez-pay.txt` (LEZ daily charges, with the Mayor's notice of the Euro VI standard), `browser/gb-tfl-tunnels.txt` (Blackwall and Silvertown tunnels: TfL's payment page, vehicle classes and the press release with the charges before and from 21 September 2026), `browser/at-nehg-10.txt` (NEHG 2022 § 10 (1), consolidated). Read again in Chrome when the charges change.
   - **When to read them again**: `check_browser.py` knows each capture's yearly change day or review cycle (e.g. the Warnowtunnel's Anlage each autumn, the M6 Toll quarterly, Greek and Liefkenshoek tolls each January) and warns from its header's read date; the weekly *Pages read in Chrome* workflow fails when one is due, so GitHub mails the owner.
   - **NV Tunnel Liefkenshoek**: a bot check refuses the workflow; the tariffs page and the FAQ's category answer were opened in Chrome and saved as `browser/be-liefkenshoek.txt` (each year's table as shown). Read again when the next year's tariffs appear.
   - **Greece**: `gr.py` reads Egnatia Odos and Attiki Odos from fetch.py's `gr-egnatia.html` and `gr-attiki.html`; if those aren't fetched or carry no prices, from `browser/gr-egnatia.txt` and `browser/gr-attiki.txt` (the pages read in Chrome; current year only). Read again when the prices change (each January).
   - **M6 Toll**: the pricing page's zone map is drawn by its script, so the workflow's copy lacks it; the map was opened in Chrome and each junction's zone and entry/exit by direction saved as `browser/gb-m6toll-zones.txt`. `m6toll.py` reads it with fetch.py's `gb-m6toll.html`, `gb-m6toll-tables.html` and `gb-m6toll-osm.json`.
   - **Landesrecht Mecklenburg-Vorpommern**: the portal is a script-driven app and the Warnowtunnel's own site has a certificate for another name; the Warnow-QuerungMautHVO M-V's Gesamtausgabe was opened in Chrome and its text saved as `browser/de-warnow-mauthvo.txt` (the Anlage as a `|` table). Read again when the change history lists a new Anlage (each autumn).
   - **Sweden**: Riksdagen's open-data text of Lag (1997:1137) and the PDF of SFS 2025:918 (the year's amounts in kronor, converted with `pdftotext -layout`).
   - **Luxembourg**: Legilux's file of the consolidated RGD du 21 mai 2019 (the page `…/a353/consolide/20241224` loads it from `/filestore/…-fr-html.html`).
   - **UK**: GOV.UK's guidance on the HGV levy service, which tabulates the levy's rates in force. legislation.gov.uk's own pages of the Act can't be kept on `tables-out`: GitHub's secret scanning rejects a key-like string in them.
   - **Norway**: the NVDB API's toll stations (object type 45, with properties, location, geometry and version dates; `fetch.py` sends an `X-Client` header, which the API asks for) and the road link sequences they are placed on (`/vegnett/api/v4/veglenkesekvenser`, 50 at a time).
   - **Ireland**: TII's page for each toll road ("Toll Locations and Charges"), and the toll booths and main roads of Geofabrik's Ireland and Northern Ireland OpenStreetMap extract (filtered with `osmium`; its date saved as `ie-osm-timestamp.txt`).
   - **Swedish bridges**: Riksdagen's text of Förordning (2014:1564), Transportstyrelsen's page for each bridge, and the toll gantries named after the bridges in Geofabrik's Sweden OpenStreetMap extract.
   - **North Macedonia**: the PDFs of the toll decision (Службен весник 118/2019, for the categories) and its 2025 amendment, whose toll table is an image: transcribed by hand into `transcribed/mk-2025.json` with the PDF's sha256, which `check_transcribed.py` compares with the fetched PDF; and the toll booths ("Патарина <station>") of Geofabrik's North Macedonia OpenStreetMap extract.
   - **Montenegro**: Monteput's price pages for the motorway and the Sozina tunnel, and the toll booths and motorway junctions of Geofabrik's Montenegro OpenStreetMap extract.
   - **Greece**: the toll price pages of Olympia Odos, Nea Odos, Kentriki Odos, Moreas and Aegean Motorway and Gefyra's Rio–Antirrio bridge prices, and the toll booths and motorway ways of Geofabrik's Greece OpenStreetMap extract. (`fetch.py` gunzips a response a server sends compressed unasked.)
   - **Swedish congestion tax**: Lag (2004:629) om trängselskatt from the Riksdag's open data (the .text form), and Transportstyrelsen's toll gantries in Geofabrik's Sweden extract.
   - **Portugal**: IMT's "Taxas de portagem" page, and the motorways and motorway junctions of Geofabrik's Portugal extract.
   - **Spain (SEITT)**: SEITT's 2026 tariff PDFs for AP-36, AP-41 and AP-7 Cartagena–Vera, and the toll booths, motorways and links of Geofabrik's Madrid, Castilla-La Mancha, Murcia and Andalucía extracts.
   - **Turkey**: KGM's 2026 bridge and motorway tariffs (PDFs linked from its "Otoyol ve Köprü Geçiş Ücretleri" page; the workflow also writes each one's word boxes with `pdftotext -bbox`), and Geofabrik's Turkey OpenStreetMap extract: the ways named after the bridges, and the toll booths, gantries and motorway junctions.
   - **Hungary**: the Nemzeti Jogszabálytár's consolidated text of 25/2013. (V. 31.) NFM rendelet. The site sends long parts (the annexes) separately; `fetch.py` asks for them as its page script does (`/ajax/njtGetBlock.json`).
2. **Build**:
   - `ro.py` → `rates/data/ro-<date>.json`: lei/km including 21% VAT by mass category, road category and emission class, from the offer JSON; the date and order number from the offer's name.
   - `dk_bridges.py` → `rates/data/dk-bridges-<year>-01-01.json`: Storebælt's express prices for trucks by length (including 25% VAT) and its green discount; Øresund's normal and ØresundBUSINESS prices (excluding VAT).
   - `frejus.py` → `rates/data/frejus-<date>.json`: one-way class 3 and 4 prices for trucks (EURO 5-6), a crossing priced at the French plaza.
   - `mont_blanc.py` → `rates/data/mont-blanc-<date>.json`: one-way class 3 and 4 prices (France to Italy; Italy to France is the same before VAT), a crossing priced at the French plaza.
   - `lv.py` → `rates/data/lv-<date>.json`: Annex 2's 1-day rates by mass, axles and EURO class (zero-emission as CO2 class 5), a `per_day` table; the date from the annex heading.
   - `ee.py` → `rates/data/ee-<date>.json`: § 190^3's 24-hour rates, each paragraph read for its mass, axles and EURO class (zero-emission as CO2 class 5); valid from the latest entry-into-force date in § 190^3.
   - `lt.py` → `rates/data/lt-<date>.json` from `browser/lt-447.txt`: point 1's daily rates for N2 and N3 by the motor vehicle's axles and EURO class, a `per_day` and `motor_axles` table; dates from the edition line; point 6's roads.
   - `se.py` → `rates/data/se-<year>-01-01.json`: the förordning's daily amounts (the last number of each row) by axles, CO2 class and EURO class.
   - `lu.py` → `rates/data/lu-<date>.json`: art. 1er 2°'s daily amounts ("Pour une journée") by CO2 class, EURO class and axles; the date from "À compter du"; the annex's roads.
   - `gb.py` → `rates/data/gb-<date>.json`: the daily rates by weight band, Euro 6 or older; zero-emission exempt; the date from "Levy rates from".
   - `no.py` → `rates/data/no-<date>.json`: one toll point per AutoPASS station with its large-vehicle price, rush-hour price, charged heading (the metering direction of its road link at the station, turned round for "Mot metrering") and hour-rule group; dated by the stations' latest version date. It prints the stations left out and why.
   - `ie.py` → `rates/data/ie-<year>-01-01.json`: one toll point per plaza with its heavy-vehicle prices; each plaza is the OSM toll booths on its road's main carriageway (ramp booths left out), the road given per page in the script.
   - `se_bridges.py` → `rates/data/se-bridges-<date>.json`: one toll point per bridge (its gantries' centre) with the heavy-truck amount; valid from Förordning (2024:194)'s entry into force.
   - `mk.py` → `rates/data/mk-<date>.json`: one toll point per booth with the category III and IV euro prices; a booth on a one-way carriageway charges its direction, booths charging both ways are merged.
   - `me.py` → `rates/data/me-2022-07-21.json`: the motorway's entry–exit prices (the calculator's `items` in the page) as a closed toll system, and the Sozina tunnel as a toll point; stations are the booths named "Naplatna stanica <name>", else the booths within 1 km of the junction of that name.
   - `gr.py` → `rates/data/gr-2026-01-01.json`: one toll point per booth (a station's two-way booths merged) with the category 3 and 4 prices divided by 1.24; page names matched to the OSM booths ("Μετωπικά/Πλευρικά Διόδια <name>") without accents, nominatives put in the genitive by a table in the script.
   - `se_congestion.py` → `rates/data/se-congestion-{stockholm,goteborg}-<date>.json`: the amount schedules of bilaga 1 (inner city, Essingeleden; low and high season) and bilaga 2, the caps, and the rules on eves, July and Göteborg's 60 minutes, each checked against the law's wording; Transportstyrelsen's gantries from OSM as toll points by schedule.
   - `pt.py` → `rates/data/pt-<year>-01-01.json`: IMT's lanços laid along each motorway's longest carriageway in OSM by their lengths (ends at junction nodes, best fit either way; interchange ends checked against the other motorway), a toll point in each lanço's middle with its class 2–4 rates divided by 1.23; the Lusoponte bridges northbound at their main span, by 1.06.
   - `es_seitt.py` → `rates/data/es-seitt-<year>-01-01.json`: each SEITT PDF's three matrices read from word boxes (Telepeaje and Efectivo sub-columns), Pesados 1 and 2 cash prices and tag prices divided by 1.21, free 00:00–05:59; stations placed at OSM toll booth clusters whose nearby links are signposted to them (one candidate each, by elimination, checked to lie between their neighbours).
   - `es_ministry.py` → `rates/data/es-ap66-<date>.json`, `es-ap46-<date>.json`, `es-ap7ac-<date>.json` from the Ministry of Transport's tables of the tolls in force (`es-min-<motorway>-<year>.txt`, on its CDN, 2025 and 2026): AP-66 as a closed system by pair of stations, placed at the toll plazas and accesses of Aucalsa's own interactive map (its Google My Maps as KML, `es-map-aucalsa.html`); AP-46 and AP-7 Alicante–Cartagena as barriers with low and high season prices (`high_rate`, `high_season`), placed at OpenStreetMap's toll plazas (`es-min-<motorway>-osm.json`). AP-9, AP-53 and AP-68 are fetched but not built: their stations can't yet be placed from an official map.
   - `m6toll.py` → `rates/data/gb-m6toll-<date>.json`: the M6 Toll as a closed system priced by zones (see the M6 Toll note above); stations are the middle of each carriageway's stretch between junctions, with the carriageway's heading, matched within 0.1 km.
   - `ba.py` → `rates/data/ba-<date>.json` from the FBiH Government's decree SN 86/20 (`ba-uredba-86-20.txt`): per direction, Sarajevo–Zenica and Sarajevo–Tarčin as directed closed systems, Svilaj and Ljubuški as mainline barriers; stations at JP Autoceste FBiH's OSM toll booths (`ba-osm.json`); KM converted at the fixed 1.95583 per euro.
   - `tr.py` → `rates/data/tr-<date>.json`: a toll point per carriageway at the middle of each bridge's main span (prices from the pdftotext lines "<class> … <price>"), and a closed system per motorway from its matrix (`tr_matrix.py` reads the word boxes: class rows, price columns, the header words over each column); stations placed at the OSM booths of their name (else the junction), the shortest chain where a name matches several, each booth serving one motorway; prices divided by 1.20. The 1915 Çanakkale bridge's tariff is checked against Malkara–Çanakkale's Gelibolu Güney – bridge price.
   - `hu.py` → `rates/data/hu-<date>.json` and `tools/roads/hu-annex1.json`: § 1 (2)-(4a) (infrastructure charge), annex 1 (tolled sections, with each one's suburban share) and annex 2 (external-cost charge).
3. **Transcribed images**: Atlandes (A63) and ALBEA (A150) publish their prices only as images. Those are transcribed by hand into `tools/fr/transcribed/*.json`, each with the image's URL and sha256; the workflow fetches the images (`sources.json`) and `check_transcribed.py` says whether each is still the one transcribed.
4. **Compare**: the workflow prints, for each rebuilt file, whether it is the same as the committed one, or the difference. A new file means a new price list: copy it into `rates/data` (with an end date on the previous one if they overlap).

The Danish plazas' coordinates are fixed in `dk_bridges.py` (OpenStreetMap toll booths). The Hungarian EUR/HUF rate (388.60, 209/2013. Korm. rendelet § 3/A) and the HU-GO checks are in `hu.py`.
