What is vehicle tax?
Vehicle tax must be paid for vehicles that are registered in and in operation in Finland. Traficom is responsible for collecting vehicle tax.
Time-based road user charges (vignettes), distance-based road tolls and vehicle emission stickers are not used in Finland.
