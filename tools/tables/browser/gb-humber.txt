Toll Prices
The Humber Bridge is a toll bridge, which means that you need to pay a toll every time you cross – though motorcycles and approved concessions go free.
The amount you pay depends on your vehicle type, so please check your vehicle classification below before travelling. Account holders also benefit from discounted toll rates.
Vehicle Type | Toll (without an account) | Toll (with an account)
Motorbikes and Trikes | Free | Free
2 axle vehicles under 3.5 tonnes: cars, small vans, and caravans | £2.00 | £1.50
2 axle vehicles between 3.5 - 7 tonnes, 9-16 passenger mini bus, bus/coaches, agricultural vehicles | £5.00 | £4.00
3 axles or more, over 7.5 tonnes | £15.00 | £12.00
All crossings must be paid by midnight the day after crossing. You can pay your toll after crossing or up to 12 months before.
