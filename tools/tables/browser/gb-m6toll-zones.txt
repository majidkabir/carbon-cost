M6toll zones explained
The M6toll is split into three zones, and costs are calculated based on how many zones you pass through. The map below shows you how the road is sectioned so you can see how many zones you might pass through on your journey.

Where do I pay?
There are main carriageway ('mainline') toll plazas between junctions T6 and T7 (northbound), and between junctions T4 and T3 (southbound). If you are exiting at junctions T3, T4, T5 or T6, the ('junction') toll plazas are situated just after you leave the main carriageway.
