The toll for the Tamar Bridge is payable one way, on the trip from Saltash (Cornwall) to Plymouth (Devon).
MGW under 3.5 tonnes 2 axles Cash/Card Single £3.00 + Trailer £6.00 TamarTag Single £1.50 + Trailer £3.00
MGW over 3.5 tonnes 2 axles Cash/Card Single £7.30 + Trailer £14.60 TamarTag Single £3.65 + Trailer £7.30
MGW over 3.5 tonnes 3 axles Cash/Card Single £12.00 + Trailer £24.00 TamarTag Single £6.00 + Trailer £12.00
MGW over 3.5 tonnes 4+ axles Cash/Card Single £16.50 + Trailer £33.00 TamarTag Single £8.25 + Trailer £16.50
