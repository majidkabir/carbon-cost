## TfL: Paying the Blackwall and Silvertown tunnels charge
Tunnel charges increased on 21 September 2026. See the current charges for your vehicle type. Charging times have not changed.
Charges apply from 06.00 - 22.00, 7 days a week in both directions.
For customers registered for Auto Pay, off-peak rates apply most of the time, including weekends. A peak charge applies during the busiest times, which are 06:00-10:00 northbound and 16:00-19:00 southbound, weekdays only.
Charges apply on all bank holidays, except Christmas Day (December 25).
Tunnel charges increased on 21 September 2026. Current charges are listed below:
Charges are per tunnel crossing
Motorcycles, mopeds, motor tricycles £1.55 £2.60 £2.60
Cars and small vans £1.55 £4.20 £4.20
Large vans £2.60 £6.80 £6.80
Heavy goods vehicles £5.25 £10.50 £10.50
