# Autostrade per l'Italia: the toll at Italian open-system barriers, from its "Calcola il pedaggio da casello a casello" form (Info viabilità).
# Page: https://viabilita.autostrade.it/it/viabilita/traffico (form "Calcola il pedaggio da casello a casello"; a barrier as the departure, no arrival).
# The form's toll service (https://d27zlgkawwz6vy.cloudfront.net/sitoaspi-prod/tolling/) refuses scripted requests (status 403), so the page was opened in Chrome and each barrier's tolls read on 2026-11-20.
# Stations as the site's station file names them (https://viabilita.autostrade.it/json/caselli.json): network c_ret, station c_stz, road. Tolls in euro by class (A, B, 3, 4, 5 axles), as the form shows them.
# Avigliana (SITAF) is read as a check: SITAF's own tariffs page gives the same amounts.
# Settimo Torinese is the Tangenziale di Torino's toll at the A5's Torino Nord barrier (its toll points are there): Ivrea Torino Piacenza's toll calculator (https://www.itpspa.it/en/?p=8466) gives Torino Nord-Settimo Nord exactly these amounts, and Torino Nord-Quincinetto as Settimo Nord-Quincinetto plus these (2026-11-20).
# Villanova (network 34, the open station beside the A21's closed Villanova barrier) is the toll of the open Torino-Villanova stretch, collected at that barrier: Ivrea Torino Piacenza's toll calculator charges Villanova B.-Asti Ovest class 5 EUR 7.90 (these 2.90 and the km), and Villanova B.-Felizzano as Villanova B.-Asti Ovest plus Asti Ovest-Felizzano, to 10 cents (2026-11-20).
